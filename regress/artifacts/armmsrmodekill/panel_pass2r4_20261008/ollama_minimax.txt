**Closure rule (2), MSR path, msr wide decline — REASONED**
The text claims "31:8 + I/F at msr wide" and "every non-mode bit except T differs" for the 0x00000014 over 0xffffffd1 decline. They don't. XOR = 0xffffffc5. Bits 31:8, 2, 0 differ. Bits 7, 6 (I, F) and 5 (T) and 4, 3, 1 (mode) are identical in both words. So the closure rule is technically violated for I/F at this specific decline — a bug that took I/F from the old CPSR rather than the operand would be invisible here.

But I/F ARE covered on the MSR path: the kill rows exercise `msr cpsr_c` with I/F deliberately differing between source and operand, and the msr_imm write logic is the same `cpsr = (cpsr & ~mask) | (operand & mask)` regardless of which field is named (after the decline clears the mode bits from `mask`). So the coverage is complete; the record is just wrong about where it lives. Suggested correction: drop "I/F" from the msr wide bullet and add a one-line note that I/F on fsxc are covered by the kill rows on c.
