#### 2 | The 2026-09-09 Label Clause Closure
**Answer:** The rowed product `{fiq, irq, abt, und, svc} x {declined, accepted} x {dpi, pop}` is the correct closure for the mode selector axis.

- **Domain closure:** In the ARM architecture, exactly five modes possess an SPSR: FIQ (0x11), IRQ (0x12), SVC (0x13), ABT (0x17), and UND (0x1b). USR (0x10) and SYS (0x1f) do not have an SPSR; attempting SPSR operations in USR/SYS is handled by the pre-#562 CPU stop paths ([cpu_arm_instr.c:1814-1818](file:///C:/DocumentNoSnc/cc/GXEMUL/GXEMUL-SEC/src/cpus/cpu_arm_instr.c#L1814-L1818)).
- **Complete matrix:** All 20 cells (`5 modes x 2 outcomes x 2 restore instructions`) are actively exercised and verified in the 10 `src *` rows ([arm_msrmode_probe.py:762-794](file:///C:/DocumentNoSnc/cc/GXEMUL/GXEMUL-SEC/regress/arm_msrmode_probe.py#L762-L794)). Each row checks whole-word CPSR readback (`c00000<mode>` for declined, `c000001f` for accepted), verifying that N|Z flags are propagated and the current mode selector correctly flows into `new_cpsr`.
- **Asymmetry analysis:** 
  - The zero-SPSR cell (`mode 0x00`) is rowed only for SVC/DPI ([arm_msrmode_probe.py:660-665](file:///C:/DocumentNoSnc/cc/GXEMUL/GXEMUL-SEC/regress/arm_msrmode_probe.py#L660-L665)). This is legitimate: testarm boots in SVC32 with all SPSRs zero-filled by `calloc` at `cpu_new`; mode 0x00 exercises the identical predicate branch in `arm_mode_is_modelled()` as mode 0x14, and this asymmetry is honestly disclosed in PRETABLE2 item 1 ([brief_sent.md:104-106](file:///C:/DocumentNoSnc/cc/GXEMUL/GXEMUL-SEC/_scratchpad/panel_20261007_230103/brief_sent.md#L104-L106)).
  - Latch state x label: `cpu->cd.arm.mode_warned` is a unified scalar bitfield on `struct arm_cpu` ([cpu_arm.h:207](file:///C:/DocumentNoSnc/cc/GXEMUL/GXEMUL-SEC/src/include/cpu_arm.h#L207)), not a per-mode table. `latch rpt` visits all 5 sites twice to verify latch suppression and independence; crossing all 5 mode labels with latch state is not architecturally meaningful because no per-mode latch bits exist.
