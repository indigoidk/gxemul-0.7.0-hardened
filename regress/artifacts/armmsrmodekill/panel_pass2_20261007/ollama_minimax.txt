## 1. Premise audit — what is wrong

### Defect B (mutant description vs. measurement)
`M_shared_latch` is described as "Save and Load sharing one latch bit". On the new probe it REDS = `dbg both, latch ind`. The `latch ind` row tests the three **decline** sites (msr/pop/dpi) having distinct bits. The five bits are defined in the header as `MSR=0x01, POP=0x02, DPI=0x04, SAVE=0x08, LOAD=0x10`. A narrow "SAVE/LOAD share" mutation (e.g. both map to 0x08) leaves 0x01/0x02/0x04 untouched, so the three decline sites still announce distinctly and the row should pass. For `latch ind` to red, the mutant must also collapse the decline bits. The name is therefore narrower than the measured effect — the floor check for "Save and Load sharing" is actually exercising a broader mutant, and the detector's ability to catch a *narrow* SAVE/LOAD share is not demonstrated. *(REASONED from the bit layout and the row's `declines=True` classify.)*

## 3. Smallest edit to the FIX that still passes all 26 rows
**Drop the per-CPU latching entirely** — delete the `mode_warned` field, the five `ARM_MODEWARN_*` macros, and the five `if (!(… & ARM_MODEWARN_*))` guards around the `fatal()` calls. Leave the `fatal()`s unconditional. The decline arithmetic (`mask &= ~ARM_FLAG_MODE`, `new_cpsr = (new_cpsr & ~ARM_FLAG_MODE) | cur_mode`) stays.

Why every row stays green:
- The classify `announced(t, once_key)` is a **substring** check, so printing every time still sets the `once` tag.
- The `latch ind` row's `declines=True` channel just needs the three text fragments to appear; they now appear every time, so `msr pop dpi` is still produced.
- The `dbg *` rows' `backstops=True` channel needs the two backstop text fragments; both still appear.

**The row that stays green as a canary is `latch ind`** — it is the row specifically built around the latch mechanism, and it passes trivially once the latch is gone. Note: the *mutants* `M_shared_*` are no longer distinguishable from the fix, so this edit sacrifices detector power; it is the smallest passing edit, not the best one.

## One thing found while verifying
The probe header claims "`MSR CPSR_c, #0` is BANNED (26-bit hole, a different defect)" was the *old* text and that "MODES 0x00-0x0F ARE NOW CLAIMED." This is consistent with the helper (`0x00` falls into `default: return 0`) and the `zero spsr` row. But the `NOT CLAIMED, disclosed` list then says "unmodelled values other than 0x00, 0x14, 0x15, 0x1e … escapes" — note `0x16, 0x18–0x1a, 0x1c–0x1d, 0x01–0x0f` are all *also* unmodelled and not rowed. The `kill 1e` row pins 0x1e via the range mutant, and `zero spsr` pins 0x00, so the helper is rowed at two points (0x00 and 0x1e) plus 0x14/0x15 from the kill rows. The disclosure correctly admits the gap, but the `MODES 0x00-0x0F ARE NOW CLAIMED` line in the header is louder than the disclosure — it could mislead a reader into thinking 0x01–0x0f are individually covered. *(REASONED.)*
