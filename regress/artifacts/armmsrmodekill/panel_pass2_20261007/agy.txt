#### 2. Caller Control Flow Verification & Unhandled Escapes
Every guard was analyzed against its surrounding control flow in [`GXEMUL-SEC`](file:///C:/DocumentNoSnc/cc/GXEMUL/GXEMUL-SEC):

1. **`msr_imm` ([`src/cpus/cpu_arm_instr.c:839-866`](file:///C:/DocumentNoSnc/cc/GXEMUL/GXEMUL-SEC/src/cpus/cpu_arm_instr.c#L839-L866)):**
   - The guard `if ((mask & ARM_FLAG_MODE) && !arm_mode_is_modelled(ic->arg[0]))` clears the mode bits (`mask &= ~ARM_FLAG_MODE;`) *prior* to computing `switch_register_banks`.
   - `switch_register_banks` evaluates to false (`0`), preventing calls to both [`arm_save_register_bank()`](file:///C:/DocumentNoSnc/cc/GXEMUL/GXEMUL-SEC/src/cpus/cpu_arm.c#L548) and [`arm_load_register_bank()`](file:///C:/DocumentNoSnc/cc/GXEMUL/GXEMUL-SEC/src/cpus/cpu_arm.c#L604).

2. **`arm_pop` ([`src/cpus/cpu_arm_instr.c:1826-1861`](file:///C:/DocumentNoSnc/cc/GXEMUL/GXEMUL-SEC/src/cpus/cpu_arm_instr.c#L1826-L1861)):**
   - Guarded after SPSR extraction and after the USR/SYS check.
   - If `!arm_mode_is_modelled(new_cpsr)`, the mode in `new_cpsr` is overwritten with the current CPSR mode: `new_cpsr = (new_cpsr & ~ARM_FLAG_MODE) | (cpu->cd.arm.cpsr & ARM_FLAG_MODE);`.
   - `switch_register_banks` becomes `0`.
   - Crucially, there is **no early return**: execution proceeds to [`lines 1851-1861`](file:///C:/DocumentNoSnc/cc/GXEMUL/GXEMUL-SEC/src/cpus/cpu_arm_instr.c#L1851-L1861) where the PC load `cpu->pc = cpu->cd.arm.r[ARM_PC] & 0xfffffffc;` and translation pointer update `quick_pc_to_pointers_arm(cpu);` execute properly.
3. **DPI S-to-PC ([`src/cpus/cpu_arm_instr_dpi.c:215-250`](file:///C:/DocumentNoSnc/cc/GXEMUL/GXEMUL-SEC/src/cpus/cpu_arm_instr_dpi.c#L215-L250)):**
   - `cpu->pc = (uint32_t)c64;` is assigned unconditionally at [`line 215`](file:///C:/DocumentNoSnc/cc/GXEMUL/GXEMUL-SEC/src/cpus/cpu_arm_instr_dpi.c#L215) prior to SPSR handling.
   - `spsr_copied` tracks whether the mode has an SPSR (0 for USR/SYS/unmodelled).
   - If `spsr_copied && !arm_mode_is_modelled(new_cpsr)`, the current mode is kept.
   - `cpu->cd.arm.cpsr = new_cpsr;` is written first, followed by `cpu->cd.arm.flags = cpu->cd.arm.cpsr >> 28;` ([`line 248`](file:///C:/DocumentNoSnc/cc/GXEMUL/GXEMUL-SEC/src/cpus/cpu_arm_instr_dpi.c#L248)), then [`arm_load_register_bank(cpu);`](file:///C:/DocumentNoSnc/cc/GXEMUL/GXEMUL-SEC/src/cpus/cpu_arm_instr_dpi.c#L249).

   - Both `default:` cases in [`arm_save_register_bank()`](file:///C:/DocumentNoSnc/cc/GXEMUL/GXEMUL-SEC/src/cpus/cpu_arm.c#L582-L597) and [`arm_load_register_bank()`](file:///C:/DocumentNoSnc/cc/GXEMUL/GXEMUL-SEC/src/cpus/cpu_arm.c#L638-L653) replace `fatal(...); exit(1);` with a latched warning and `return;`.
