#### 3. Questions & Review-Asks Responses

##### Q1: Premise Audit
Answered in sections 1 and 2 above. A reserved mode reaches CPSR strictly via `msr_imm`/`msr`, or via restore from SPSR in `bdt_load` / DPI S-to-PC after SPSR has been poisoned by `msr_imm_spsr`/`msr_spsr`.

##### Q2: The Disposition
- **For `msr_imm` / `msr`**: In ARMv5 ARM DDI 0100I §A4.1.40, MSR has "Exceptions: None". Writing a reserved value to M[4:0] is architecturally UNPREDICTABLE. Declining only the mode change (`mask &= ~ARM_FLAG_MODE`) is the correct disposition:
  - Other requested bits (e.g., flags from field `f`, interrupt masks $I/F$ from field `c`) continue to apply normally.
- **For Callers 2 & 3 (`bdt_load` & DPI S-to-PC)**: In ARM DDI 0100I §A4.1.22 and §A4.1.27, restoring an unallocated mode from SPSR is also UNPREDICTABLE with "Exceptions: None". Injecting FAULT (`ARM_EXCEPTION_UND`) is architecturally ungrounded and will panic kernels without an unallocated-mode UND vector. Declining the mode switch while completing the PC and flag transfer preserves emulator integrity.
- **For `msr_imm_spsr`**: Masking out reserved mode bits when writing SPSR stops poison injection at the source.

##### Q3: Where the Decline Lives
- **Guards in Save/Load with rollback**: Fails. By the time `arm_load_register_bank()` runs, the old mode is already overwritten in CPSR. In Caller 3, `arm_save_register_bank()` is called unconditionally *before* SPSR is inspected. Caller 4 would crash in Save on any prior unhandled reserved mode.
- **Shared helper before mode update**: Complete success. With `arm_mode_is_modelled(mode)`:
  - In `msr_imm`: If `!arm_mode_is_modelled(new_val & ARM_FLAG_MODE)`, clear `ARM_FLAG_MODE` from `mask`. `switch_register_banks` evaluates to 0, Save and Load are skipped, and non-mode bits are applied safely.
  - In `bdt_load`: Check `new_cpsr`. If unmodelled, preserve current mode in `new_cpsr` and set `switch_register_banks = 0`.
  - In DPI S-to-PC: Gate bank switching and mode overwrite on `arm_mode_is_modelled(spsr_mode)`.
