## 1. Premise audit (Q1)

**WRONG (READ): "the six modelled modes."** The inlined switches contain **seven** case labels — USR32(0x10), FIQ32(0x11), IRQ32(0x12), SVC32(0x13), ABT32(0x17), UND32(0x1B), SYS32(0x1F). USR and SYS share an arm; that makes six *arms/banks*, seven *modes*. This is load-bearing: a helper written to "six" misclassifies SYS32, and the probe's own `acc …sys` row (which accepts seven modes: usr + fiq/irq/abt/und/sys + `same svc`) would go red on a legal guest MSR to SYS.

**Verified by enumeration (REASONED from the packet's word): the trigger claim holds.** 0xE321F014: cond=E(AL); bits 27–23 = 00110 → MSR(immediate); bit22=0 → CPSR; bits 21–20 = 10; bits 19–16 = 0001 → field mask `c`; bits 15–12 = 1111 (SBZ as required); rotate=0; imm8=0x14 → immediate 0x14. 0x14 = 0b10100 ∉ {0x10,0x11,0x12,0x13,0x17,0x1B,0x1F} → reserved. Note also (READ) that `msr_imm` masks only USR32, not SYS32 — correct, SYS is privileged — so the trigger fires from SYS too.

**WRONG as derived, right in conclusion (READ+REASONED): §2's member-1 bullet.** "Silent wrong-bank execution, then the next switch hits Save's default" fails twice under member 1 as stated ("just drop the exit(1)"): (a) it is not silent — per the packet's own carrier rule, `fatal()` still prints on every hit; (b) the next Save-default hit does **not** kill, because member 1 as the fix neuters both defaults — the machine prints, falls through, and self-heals into the new mode with a stale bank (the reserved period's r13/r14 were stored nowhere). ## 2. Disposition per caller (Q2) — and the seven members

MSR: decline the M-field only; **all other fields in the same write still apply** (f-field flags, I, F, T). REASONED: the UNPREDICTABLE is scoped to the reserved mode *value*, not the instruction, and MSR lists "Exceptions: None"; whole-write decline would also discard legal flag/I/F/T writes bundled with a bad mode. LDM^ / DPI-S-to-PC: restore CPSR from the SPSR with the mode bits forced to the current mode; keep the register loads and the PC transfer; keep the SPSR's T bit (it governs PC interpretation). FAULT is worse here: no architecturally indicated UNDEF exists for the restore, and synthesizing one mid-exception-return corrupts the guest's exception state a second time. arm_exception: no guard (producer asserted modelled — flagged above). Member disposal: 1 rejected (stale-bank corruption, and the print spam survives); 2 rejected vs 3 (loses all diagnostic signal on an UNPREDICTABLE a real guest may hit; a once-print is free); **3 accepted (refined)**; 4 meaningless (no defined semantics to accept); 5 rejected ("Exceptions: None"); 6 rejected (you cannot "back" a guest-driven process kill as semantics — that is the row's bug); 7 rejected (UNPREDICTABLE has nothing to implement; a bankless pseudo-mode adds untestable semantics). **No eighth member**: the nearest candidate, SANITIZE (force a chosen modelled mode), is a granularity variant of decline and dominated — keeping the current mode keeps r13/r14 banked reality consistent with what the guest has been executing.

## 3. Where the decline lives (Q3)

Helper at each guest mode-write, plus backstop defaults; **not** rollback-inside-Load (Load cannot know the old mode once CPSR is overwritten, and the decision becomes invisible to the write's other fields). Sketch against post-Load code: `msr_imm`'s `flags = cpsr >> 28` (line 844) is mode-independent ✓; callers 2/3's PC write and `quick_pc_to_pointers` depend on the T bit, which per-field decline preserves — a whole-write decline would misexecute a T=1 restore.

## 4. The backstop (Q4)

With all writers guarded the defaults are unreachable by construction — keep them anyway as latched announce+decline: they are the only guard against unenumerated writers (debugger poke, a fifth caller, future ARMv6 RFE/CPS/SRS). Latch state: **per-CPU field**, not function-static — a static is shared across CPU instances on multi-CPU machines and survives machine re-init; "per instance" in the house idiom should mean per CPU.

## 5. Smallest edit that breaks the fix and passes (Q5)

**Guard only caller 1 (`msr`/`msr_imm`); leave callers 2/3 unguarded.** All 17 rows drive the MSR path, so every row stays green while `MSR SPSR_c, #0x14` followed by `SUBS pc, lr, #0` still reaches Load's `exit(1)`.
