## S3 — the two interactions, against source

### Amendments 1 and 6: EOF-alone does **not** make `enc ok` / `no cbit` / dump-DEST the only backstop

Amendment 1: armed kill row, **EOF alone ⇒ RED**; the `unimplemented mode 20` line is diagnosis. Amendment 6: encoding-nop / c-bit-off / MSR-never-stepped produces **no EOF** and looks like a correct decline.

Alive-without-EOF is the POST colour. So never-fired is a false green **unless the want distinguishes decline from silence**.

Those three rows are **necessary and not sufficient**. Smallest defeat of each, if you lean on it alone:

| control as the spec named it | smallest defeat | what you actually need |
|---|---|---|
| dump DEST after `step 2` | proves MRS ran, not that the MSR word was planted or stepped | dump the **MSR slot** against the same dict entry `put w` used |
| `no cbit` (`MSR CPSR_f, #0x14`) | want is already `alive, m=13`. A decoder that **never writes the mode field** greens `no cbit` **and** every kill row | accept rows GREEN on HEAD are the execution proof `no cbit` cannot be |
| `enc ok` walking a constructor | session plants a literal (the BFX incident). Plant `0xE321F010` (USR32, modelled) under a `kill 14` label | **one `WORDS` dict**. `enc ok` iterates it. Every `put w` comes from it. Per-session plant-dump must match |

What actually closes false-green — all required:

1. **POST want is `m=13 sp once`, not `m=13`.** Member 3 prints `(once)`. Encoding-nop, skipped-step, and silent member-2 print nothing. `classify()` must put ` once` in the *got* string so a missing latch is a FAIL, not a comment.
2. **Poison `DEST+4` with `0xdeadbeef`.** After an alive MSR step, dump `DEST+4` (never `DEST+0`). Poison still there ⇒ post-MSR `MRS/STR` never ran ⇒ RED.
3. **One `WORDS` dict + plant-dump of the MSR slot.** This is the load-bearing anti-false-green, the way amendment 6 hoped `enc ok` was. Treat it with the same scrutiny as the kill rows.
4. **Named-red tally.** HEAD must be exactly the three named reds. `17/17` on HEAD is not success.

So: under amendment 1 those rows stop being conveniences **for attribution**. They are not, by themselves, the thing that stops a false green. Drop `(once)` from the want and amendment 6’s encoding-nop greens POST.

### Amendments 3 and 5: two writes do **not** make `save 2s` nondeterministic on this probe

Amendment 3 is half-right. The original two reds were `kill 14` and `kill 15` (two **Load** holes, catching `== 0x14`) — **not** Load vs Save. The spec already disclosed `save 2s` as detector-only. The underlying point stands: without a scored two-write row, a Load-only skip-memcpy POSTs clean and still dies on the write *out* of reserved mode. The round’s disposition is member 3 at **both** defaults. **Score `save 2s`. Keep `kill 15`. Do not replace one with the other.**

Amendment 5 is true of the encoding (`#0x14` clears I and F) and true of testarm (cons/ether/rtc hang off `irqc` in `default_test`). It does **not** make a stepped `save 2s` racy:

- ARM IRQ dispatch is inside `if (!single_step)` in `cpu_dyntrans.c`. `-V` sets `single_step`.
- There is no ARM FIQ dispatch in that file and no `fiq_asserted` on the ARM cpu struct. Clearing F is a no-op for delivery here.
- Debugger interaction `timer_stop()`s; `step N` does not run host-timer callbacks.
- `dump` is host `memory_rw`.

**What pins it: stay on `step`; never `continue`.** A `continue` after a *landed* `#0x14` (Load-only POST) can take `arm_exception` → Save on the reserved current mode. That is a free-run hazard, not a step hazard. Do not change the filed stimulus to `#0xD4`.
