*** THE FIX NEEDED TWO CONDITIONS, AND A THIRD FORM WAS A REGRESSION.  All of this
    is MEASURED (drainscore.sh, drainvar.py), not argued. ***

    variant                          empty  empty8  leftover  wr+pkt  ACCEPT
    -----------------------------    -----  ------  --------  ------  ------
    inbuf_len > 0 only               CLOSED CLOSED  OPEN      CLOSED  CLOSED
    bad-len decline only             CLOSED CLOSED  CLOSED    OPEN    OPEN
    both, flag a STRUCT FIELD        CLOSED CLOSED  CLOSED    CLOSED  OPEN
    both, flag a PER-ACCESS LOCAL    CLOSED CLOSED  CLOSED    CLOSED  CLOSED

Each half closes a door the other leaves open, and the reason is structural:

  * `inbuf_len > 0` misses the LEFTOVER door because an unread payload leaves
    occupancy at a genuine 512, so `512 > 0` passes.  Named by the grok seat.
  * the bad-len decline misses the WRITE+PKT door because no bad-len access
    occurs there -- a CDB dispatch consumes the LOCAL inbuf_len to a stale 0
    while pushing fresh DATA-IN into the ring.  Named by the fable seat, and
    independently by opus.

*** THE STRUCT-FIELD FORM IS WORSE THAN THE DEFECT IT FIXES, and the 45-row
    baseline could not see it. ***  A latched flag silences every later legal
    write -- corruption traded for silent data loss.  No pre-existing row performs
    a legal write AFTER a bad-len one, so "all green" was measuring the wrong
    thing.  `row_badlen_then_legal` was written for exactly that, and `M9_persist`
    is killed by it ALONE.  This is the de-escalation clause's accept side: a fix
    that removes a write owes a row proving the LEGAL write still lands.


*** THE TWO MUTANTS THAT READ AS IMPROVEMENTS, both from the opus seat, and both
    invisible to every empty-ring row. ***

M3_ptr        `inbuf_len > 0`  ->  `d->inbuf_head != d->inbuf_tail`
              Live pointers instead of a stale local.  It reads as MORE correct --
              which is what makes it dangerous.  It is invisible to every
              empty-ring row and dies only on the ATAPI fall-through.

M6_recompute  recompute occupancy from the live pointers before the guard.
              The intuitively CORRECT fix, and measurably worse: it fires on a
              2048-byte DATA-IN because 2048 % 512 == 0.

Both die on `a CDB dispatch does not drain to disk` ALONE.  The seat that named
them warned that without such a row M3_ptr "ships green while writing the CD's
payload to the IDE image".  That warning is discharged by measurement here.


*** WHAT THIS ROUND DOES NOT CLOSE -- stated because a seat measured it. ***

The RING ALIAS survives.  With #478 applied, a guest that issues WRITE SECTORS,
then a packet command returning 2048 bytes, then 256 ordinary 16-bit words of its
own, still has the image receive 512 CD-ROM payload bytes and ZERO of its own.
`wdccdbaccum` stays open with that measurement attached.

The alias root was also identified and is NOT fixed here: `wdc_command()` clears
`atapi_cmd_in_progress` and does NOT clear `write_in_progress`.  A one-line
symmetric fix closes that door, but it is a different site and a different class,
so it is filed rather than merged into this round.

The `write_count == 0` division is still a host SIGFPE if it were ever reachable.
It is not (the count is set only as `seccnt ? seccnt : 256` and the flag clears the
instant it reaches 0), and `inbuf_len > 0` does not remove it.  A `write_count > 0`
co-guard would close it for free -- which under the ADDITION LICENCE is exactly
"it cannot hurt", so it is FILED, not added.
