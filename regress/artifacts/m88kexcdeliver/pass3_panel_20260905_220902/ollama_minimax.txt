### Q3 — What did all nine of us miss?

1. **Shadow-update mutant gap.** `M_NOSHADOW` and `M_SNIPX` are in the closure denominator but absent from the v3 14-mutant list and absent from the disclosed-as-EQUIVALENT list. Either the closure claim is wrong or the disclosure is incomplete.
2. **EPSR mutant unstated in v3.** `M_EPSRAFTER` was 0/10 in pass 2 (suggesting EQUIVALENT), but v3's status is not stated. Pass-2 → v3 silent transitions are exactly the ones that hide unmeasured EQUIVALENT claims.
3. **Three pass-2 mutants vanish without trace.** `M_MODECOND 3/10`, `M_VEC7 5/10`, `M_IMPLOVF 9/10` are not in v3 and not disclosed. If consolidated into `M_ISTRAP`/`M_TRAPONLY`, say so; if EQUIVALENT, measure and disclose.
4. **`M_LEVEL` asymmetry.** 3/22 killed but 19 rows carry `bad=level`. Either the mutant is too narrow (3 of 19 expected kills land) or the probe's level check is too lenient (19 rows violate level but only 3 fail the probe). This is the one low-kill-count mutant in a table otherwise clustered at 17–22; worth a second look before declaring the level channel closed.
5. **The dslot pin is wrong-as-labelled.** Even honest, the row will FAIL on the correction, forcing a re-pin. The brief itself flags this. Not strictly a miss, but a known sharp edge.
6. **Cross-class enumeration.** `BOUNDS` and `ERROR` have no instruction-raise site by construction, so their `class × route` cells collapse to `{trap}`. The author notes this. But the `dslot` row exercises only one branch (instruction-raise in a delay slot); there is no row for a trap in a delay slot of a taken branch — that is a missing cell in the `route × delay-slot` matrix.
