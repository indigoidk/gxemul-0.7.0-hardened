vacuous by construction.

TWO ROWS THE CLOSURE DOES **NOT** BUY, and they belong in the contract as disclosed rather than left silent:

  * MXM and BO: `m88k_stcr' sets `cpu->running = false' for MXM and for a BO flip (verified by reading), so no row
