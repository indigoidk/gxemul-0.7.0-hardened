## Q2 — smallest unscreened edit, not disclosed

I cannot pin this down with the packet alone — the cells I cannot read are X6's read-data
channel (does X6 ever `memory_readmax64` at +0x180/+0x184/+0x280/+0x284 at a state where
`status != enable`?), and the exact states X9/X10 reach. Both of those decide whether the
following two candidates live or die.

**Candidate A** (high prior):

> Single character in the IRQ_STATUS read: `odata = d->irq_status & d->irq_enable;` →
> `odata = d->irq_status | d->irq_enable;` (the `&` flips to `|`).

Why it is the smallest: one character. It is on the read side of a decline-bearing arm, so
it is "the five arms". Why it might be unscreened:

- At every X14 read in the diff, `irq_status == irq_enable == 0x10`. The `|` and `&` agree
  there (0x10 | 0x10 = 0x10, 0x10 & 0x10 = 0x10).
- At every X6 read in the diff, the read-data channel is checked but the state seeds I can
  infer are {0, 0x70, 0xffffffff, "seeded"}. At 0, 0x70, and all-1s, `x|y == x&y` whenever
  `x == y` or one is 0.
- A "seeded" state may have `status != enable`. At such a state `status | enable > status & enable`,
  the bug surfaces. But I cannot see X6's seeded state from the packet.

What would settle it: X6's seeded-state value (one fact).

**Candidate B** (lower prior, more interesting):

> Single character in the IRQ_ENABLE_CLEAR drive: `if (d->irq_status & d->irq_enable)`
> → `if (d->irq_status | d->irq_enable)`.

Same logic. The Set+Clear row at state (status=0x18, enable=0x30 → 0x70 → 0x60) reaches the
deassert branch (0x18 & 0x60 == 0). With `|`, the buggy condition fires (nonzero) and
asserts instead. The row's `ZCPU(1, 1)` catches it. So this candidate is **caught** — the
two-polarity X14 coverage actually does close it.

So Candidate B is screen. Candidate A is the live one. Without X6's read-states I can't
say it's unscreened — only that it survives the X14 rows. **NOT CHECKABLE FROM PACKET**;
name the seeded states to settle.

If I have to pick one for the panel, I pick Candidate A and tag it as live-or-dead on X6.
