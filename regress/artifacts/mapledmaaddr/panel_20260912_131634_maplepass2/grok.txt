### S1 — weaken `== 0x04` without touching `0x10` or `0x80`  (the leftover hoist cell)

Exact edit, one insert above `switch (relative_addr)`:

```c
	if (relative_addr & 4)
		relative_addr = 0x04;
```

That is `& 3` → `& 1` on an equality: `relative_addr == 0x04` becomes `(relative_addr & 4) == 4`, then forced into the existing arm. Preimage includes `0x0c`, **`0x14` (ENABLE)**, `0x1c`, **`0x8c` (RESET)**, `0xe4`, … and does **not** include RESET2 (`0x10 & 4 == 0`) or SPEED (`0x80 & 4 == 0`).

| `other read` | reads `+0x10`, still RESET2, still 0. Measured under M_HOIST as the *total* hoist only (`probe_M_HOIST.raw`: `other read 0x8c012345` / want `0x0`) |

Same cell, two other spellings, also 16/16: `if (relative_addr == 0x14) relative_addr = 0x04;` (ENABLE only); `case 0x04: case 0x14:` with ENABLE’s own `case` removed (C refuses a duplicate label).
