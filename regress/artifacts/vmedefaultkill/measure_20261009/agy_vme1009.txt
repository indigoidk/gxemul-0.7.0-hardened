### 5. Flags

FLAG: Under FAULT, while [`m88k_exception`](file:///C:/DocumentNoSnc/cc/GXEMUL/GXEMUL-SEC/src/cpus/cpu_m88k.c#L908-L921) updates the MC88100 data unit control registers (DMT0=0x0000413d for reads, DMT0=0x0000403f for writes, DMD0=data, DMA0=target offset), it never touches the MC88200 data CMMU registers; a guest `ld` of PFSR (`0xfff7f108`) and PFAR (`0xfff7f10c`) returns `0x00000000`.
FLAG: Because Data CMMU PFSR reads `0x00000000` (`CMMU_PFSR_SUCCESS`), the guest kernel fault handler in [`eh.S`](file:///C:/DocumentNoSnc/cc/GXEMUL/_guest-src-openbsd22/sys/arch/mvme88k/mvme88k/eh.S#L1017) records `dpfsr=0`, preventing [`trap.c`](file:///C:/DocumentNoSnc/cc/GXEMUL/_guest-src-openbsd22/sys/arch/mvme88k/mvme88k/trap.c#L319) from identifying the condition as a bus error (`0x3`), which causes an unexpected kernel panic via `panictrap()` during autoconfiguration.
FLAG: Under FAULT, stepping 2 instructions from `0x10000` causes step 1 to take the data-access exception to `0x00000018` (`VBR + 0x18`), and step 2 attempts to execute at `0x00000018`. With uninitialized memory (zeroes) at `0x18`, GXemul encounters an unimplemented instruction (`0x00000000`), halts all CPUs, and keeps `PC=0x00000018`.
FLAG: In GXemul, VBR is initialized to `0x00000000` at machine setup, verifying that MC88100 vector 3 correctly vectors directly to physical address `0x00000018`.
FLAG: In MC88100 instruction encoding, the primary opcode occupies bits 31..26 (`op26 << 26`); control (c) unassemble validation was crucial to verify `0x14430000` (`ld r2,r3,0x0`), `0x24430000` (`st r2,r3,0x0`), and `0x58801234` (`or r4,r0,0x1234`) against hand-assembly errors.
