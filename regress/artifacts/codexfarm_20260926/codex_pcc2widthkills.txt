## 2. Required architectural/device behavior

Listing `C:\DocumentNoSnc\cc\GXEMUL\_scratchpad\*.txt` found 428 text files. The two relevant manuals are:

Directly documented:

- The PCCchip2 CSR block is at `$FFF42000` and “can be accessed” as 8-, 16-, or 32-bit cycles: `vmesbcp2.txt:4651-4656`.
- The byte-register/data-lane layout is shown at `:4695-4812`.
- Per-register definitions cover GENCTL `:4857-4870`, VECBASE `:4940-4968`, PSCALEADJ `:5141-5169`, T1/T2CTL `:5182-5253`, T1/T2ICR `:5343-5408`, SCC registers `:5413-5588`, SCSIICR `:5974-6005`, and IPL/MASK `:6419-6487`.
- MC88100 halfword/word/doubleword alignment is specified at `mc88100.txt:1727-1795`; misalignment is detected before device dispatch at `:9231-9246`.
- Its load/store descriptions are at `:5302-5349,5887-5929`. A doubleword operation is two word bus transactions, not one documented 64-bit PCC2 transaction.

Therefore:

- A host exit is indefensible.
- Turning documented 16/32-bit PCC2 cycles into guest bus faults is also wrong.
- Wide reads should compose adjacent address-order lanes.
- The lane table plus each byte register’s documented operations supports per-lane write composition as the eventual fidelity target.

The last point is an inference: the excerpts do not explicitly prescribe ordering or atomicity when one wide write spans registers with different side effects. That uncertainty explains the current conservative write decline and the separate `pcc2widecompose` row at `OUTSTANDING_BUGS.md:15400-15424`. The manual provides no warrant for a single 64-bit PCC2 callback.

## 5. Smallest controlled witness

Use the unmodified in-tree `-E mvme88k -e mvme187` machine, which is the only machine arm that installs PCC2.

With registers preloaded so `r2 = 0xfff42000`, `r3` points to writable RAM, and `r5 = 0x5a5a5a5a`, the smallest instruction that reaches a current guard is one aligned guest halfword store to T2CTL: `st.h` from `r5` to `r2 + 0x16`.

The controlled sequence is:

1. Store the marker from `r5` to RAM at `r3`.
2. Execute the T2CTL halfword store.
3. Load the RAM marker after the access and inspect it.
