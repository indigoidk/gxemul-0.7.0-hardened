WHY THIS FILE EXISTS AND WHY ITS MTIME MATTERS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The oracle-correction rule's discriminator is ORDER: re-pin the oracle, measure the
committed build RED under the new expectations, then change the code.  For
`want_rlwimi' that order is UNAVAILABLE in its usual form -- the handler repair is
correct and a corrected oracle AGREES with it, and agreement with a correct
implementation is what a right oracle and a fitted oracle both look like.

So this enumeration is written BEFORE the helper edit, and it is derived from Book I's
definition of MASK rather than from the tree, so that no line below could have been
chosen to make a row green.

1. mask32(MB, ME) -- the mask term rlwinm AND rlwimi share
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   SPACE IS 1024, NOT 4096.  MB and ME are 5-bit M-form fields (Book I :4311-4313),
   so the domain is 32 x 32.  (4096 is want_rldimi's (MB,SH) space, and transposing
   it onto this helper is an error this project made and corrected today.)

   non-wrapping MB <= ME :  528   old == new :  528   <-- MUST be all; a correction
                                                        that moves these is WRONG
   wrapping     MB >  ME :  496   old != new :  496
     of those, new high half == 0xffffffff :  496
     of those, new low  word == old value  :  496

   => the correction is exactly ADD THE HIGH HALF, CHANGE NOTHING ELSE.
   Closed form: mask_b_63(mb+32) has a zero high half; mask_0_e(me+32) has an all-ones
   high half.  AND gives 0 (low-word-confined, identical to the old loop); OR gives
   0xffffffff.  That is why the split is clean rather than statistical.

2. want_rldimi's mask -- MASK(MB, 63-SH), the md-form
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   space (MB, SH) = 64 x 64 = 4096
   non-wrapping : 2080   old == new : 2080
   wrapping     : 2016   old != new : 2016

   ALL-ZERO MASKS   old: 2016      new:    0

   *** THE OLD FORM PRODUCES AN ALL-ZERO MASK FOR 2016 OF 4096 PAIRS -- 49.2% OF THE
   *** SPACE -- AND BOOK I :4031 SAYS "There is no way to specify an all-zero mask."
   The oracle was returning the untouched destination for every one of them, and would
   therefore have AGREED with any implementation that also omitted the wrap.
