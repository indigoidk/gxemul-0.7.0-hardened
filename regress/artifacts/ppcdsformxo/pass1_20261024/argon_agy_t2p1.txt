### Q1(b) Ordering of the `imm` mask relative to `imm == 0` (`P2`)
* **Where `imm` is read (`READ`)**: In [`X(to_be_translated)`](file:///C:/DocumentNoSnc/cc/GXEMUL/GXEMUL-SEC/src/cpus/cpu_ppc_instr.c#L4295-L4351), `imm` is read twice:
  1. Line 4335: `(imm==0? 16 : 0)` inside the `ppc_loadstore[...]` index.
  2. Line 4351: `ic->arg[2] = (ssize_t)imm;`.
* **Exact inputs where raw vs. masked `imm == 0` disagree (`DERIVED` from `P2`)**: They disagree **if and only if `DS == 0` and `XO != 0`** (the three legal zero-displacement non-zero-XO cells: `ldu` with `DS=0` [`C5`, raw `imm = 1`], `lwa` with `DS=0` [`C10`, raw `imm = 2`], and `stdu` with `DS=0` [`C16`, raw `imm = 1`]). When `DS != 0`, both raw and masked `imm` are non-zero; when `XO == 0`, raw equals masked.
* **Effect of `_0` vs non-`_0` handlers (`READ`)**: Opened [cpu_ppc_instr_loadstore.c](file:///C:/DocumentNoSnc/cc/GXEMUL/GXEMUL-SEC/src/cpus/cpu_ppc_instr_loadstore.c#L41-L54) and [cpu_ppc_instr_loadstore.c](file:///C:/DocumentNoSnc/cc/GXEMUL/GXEMUL-SEC/src/cpus/cpu_ppc_instr_loadstore.c#L195-L216).
  * On a **64-bit CPU** (`!cpu->is_32bit`), [`LS_N`](file:///C:/DocumentNoSnc/cc/GXEMUL/GXEMUL-SEC/src/cpus/cpu_ppc_instr_loadstore.c#L195-L198) immediately calls `LS_GENERIC_N(cpu, ic)` and returns. Because `LS_GENERIC_N` is emitted only once per `(size, zero, load, update)` tuple under `#ifndef LS_IGNOREOFS` ([generate_ppc_loadstore.c](file:///C:/DocumentNoSnc/cc/GXEMUL/GXEMUL-SEC/src/cpus/generate_ppc_loadstore.c#L62-L82) `READ`), `ppc_instr_ldu_0` and `ppc_instr_ldu` call the same `ppc_generic_ldu`, which always adds `(int32_t)ic->arg[2]`.

### Q1(c) Illegal `XO` handling (`58/3`, `62/2`, `62/3`) and `R12` (`P6`, `S5`, `S6`, `R12`)

* **`R12` classification (`DERIVED`)**: Because architectural Program exception (`5 FAULT`) does not exist on this CPU (`R12` availability rule; `S5`, `P6`), `goto bad` is the fork's decoder-level refusal idiom (**`3 DECLINE + BOUNDED ANNOUNCE`** coupled with halting execution via `cpu->running = 0` at [`bad:`](file:///C:/DocumentNoSnc/cc/GXEMUL/GXEMUL-SEC/src/cpus/cpu_dyntrans.c#L2004-L2015) `READ`). Because `bad:` sets `cpu->running = 0` on the first hit (`S6`, `P15`), the announcement (`"UNIMPLEMENTED instruction at 0x..."`) is inherently bounded to one emission per run without a static latch.
* **Bare `goto bad` vs. `fatal()` before `goto bad` (`READ`)**: Checked every `goto bad` site in [`X(to_be_translated)`](file:///C:/DocumentNoSnc/cc/GXEMUL/GXEMUL-SEC/src/cpus/cpu_ppc_instr.c#L4531-L5426) (`READ`):

Reserved/illegal `XO` values (`58/3`, `62/2`, `62/3`) are invalid instruction forms (`S2`, `S4`), so **bare `default: goto bad;`** is the exact fork idiom.

### Q1(f) Alignment of `EA` (`P8`, `S14`)
* **Verification of `P8` (`READ`)**: Opened [cpu_ppc_instr_loadstore.c](file:///C:/DocumentNoSnc/cc/GXEMUL/GXEMUL-SEC/src/cpus/cpu_ppc_instr_loadstore.c#L41-L198) and [memory_ppc.c](file:///C:/DocumentNoSnc/cc/GXEMUL/GXEMUL-SEC/src/cpus/memory_ppc.c#L254-L296). On 64-bit CPUs (`!cpu->is_32bit`), [`LS_N`](file:///C:/DocumentNoSnc/cc/GXEMUL/GXEMUL-SEC/src/cpus/cpu_ppc_instr_loadstore.c#L195-L198) always delegates to [`LS_GENERIC_N`](file:///C:/DocumentNoSnc/cc/GXEMUL/GXEMUL-SEC/src/cpus/cpu_ppc_instr_loadstore.c#L41-L186), which computes `uint64_t addr = reg(ic->arg[1]) + (int32_t)ic->arg[2];` and passes `addr` directly to `cpu->memory_rw` with `LS_SIZE` (`4` or `8`) and no alignment check (open items `#430`/`#431`). Even on 32-bit CPUs where the fast path checks `ofs & (LS_SIZE - 1)` ([cpu_ppc_instr_loadstore.c](file:///C:/DocumentNoSnc/cc/GXEMUL/GXEMUL-SEC/src/cpus/cpu_ppc_instr_loadstore.c#L229)), an unaligned address simply falls back to `LS_GENERIC_N` (`:230`).
* **Conclusion**: Nothing in the repair or its handlers assumes a 4- or 8-byte-aligned `EA` (`RA` may hold any byte address; the displacement `EXTS(DS || 0b00)` is always a multiple of 4). Using `BASE = 0x20080` in the probe is purely a test-data choice.
