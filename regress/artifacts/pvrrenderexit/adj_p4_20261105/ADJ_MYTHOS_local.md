2. The rule: D2 for a floating-point compare.

Rule D2 as adopted (tools/CLAUDE.md:1554-1566) is stated for an integer X against a constant B and terminates by "at most two rows per compare". Here four compares and one truncation take doubles: `x1 > x2` (663), `x <= x2` (683), `x1 >= d->xsize` / `x2 >= d->xsize` (659), `vram_z[ofs] <= z` (686), `int x = x1` (683). Codex is right that no least move exists: a row at fraction f reds every move >= f and none below, so the family {c in (0,1)} has no least member and no finite row set pins it. The 2026-10-09 ruling (tools/CLAUDE.md:1618-1624) already settled what that means: "every single slice is one-row closable while the family never is" grades the FAMILY clause (e), and "closable by a finite row set" is not a floor discriminator. So option (c) is the default, and I adopt it with ONE bounded exception, which is option (a) at a resolution the function itself supplies:

RULING D2-F. (i) A position compare in a rasterizer is pinned at the PIXEL (the integer edges D2 already demands, rowed) and at the HALF PIXEL, once per compare per rasterizer, on the side the integer edge does not dominate. The half pixel is not one fraction among fractions: the body discretizes positions by truncation (683; 728 by the int parameters), and round-to-nearest is the only other discretization, which is exactly the +0.5 move at every position compare; the space of conventions has two members, the space of epsilons has none least. (ii) Below the half pixel the family is clause-(e) debt, disclosed as SAMPLED (the 10-09 vocabulary) with that derivation. (iii) The depth compare has no unit: the body never discretizes z, so there is no half unit to pin; its generating set {<, ==, >} x Z-write is the floor (P8, rowed), the negative side of `<= z - c` is dominated by the equal rows for every c > 0, and the positive side is SAMPLED at c = 1.0 and 0.5 and owed nothing finer. (iv) texturedline's ends are ints (728), so every fractional move there is EQUIVALENT: built, predicted green, named. This terminates: at most one extra row per position compare per rasterizer per Z-state, a fixed count, and nothing else can be demanded under it.
