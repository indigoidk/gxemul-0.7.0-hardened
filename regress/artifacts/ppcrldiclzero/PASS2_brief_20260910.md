## THE DEFECT

`X(rldicl)` built its mask into `tmp` -- the ROTATED VALUE -- while the destination was assigned
`tmp & tmp2`, and `tmp2` was assigned `0` just above and never assigned again. **The destination was
therefore set to ZERO on every execution, for every source, rotate amount and mask begin.** `rldicl` is
the canonical 64-bit masking and zero-extension instruction; `rldicl rA,rS,0,32` is the standard 32-bit
zero-extend that compilers emit constantly.

**WITNESSED AT RUNG 3 BEFORE THE EDIT**, on unmodified `-E bareppc`, `-E testppc` and `-E macppc -e g5`
(the Power Mac G5 model, a PPC970 and therefore 64-bit): **19/27, with 190 of 190 census cells wrong and
all 190 returning exactly zero.** After the fix, 24/27 and 0 of 190 wrong. The wants come from the
architecture manual's own RTL, not from this tree, and every encoded word was re-read by the tree's OWN
disassembler -- a different code path from the execute handler.

**TWO THINGS THE ORIGINAL READING UNDERSTATED.** (a) TWO tokens were wrong, not one: the `|=` also
destroyed the rotated data, which is unobservable only because the result was ANDed with zero. (b) There
is a SECOND channel -- the record form reports EQ on every execution, because CR0 is fed the always-zero
destination, so this corrupts CONTROL FLOW and not only data.

**AND THE OBVIOUS REPAIR IS A TRAP, BUILT AND MEASURED.** Deleting the `& tmp2` ships
`ROTL64(rS,SH) | MASK(MB,63)` -- an OR where the architecture says AND -- and gets **126 of 190 cells
wrong**. It is worse than the original defect in one specific way: it removes the always-zero signature
that makes the bug easy to spot while leaving the value wrong.
