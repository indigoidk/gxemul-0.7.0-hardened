The stepping distinction needs **three categories**:

- **Inside a guest load/store:** UART, keyboard command/reply handling, WDC, and PIC recalculation.
- **Scheduled device ticks without a host-timer prerequisite:** UART/keyboard input polling and reassertion of existing WDC state.
- **Host-timer-dependent production, followed by a device tick:** PIT and RTC. Their host callbacks increment pending counters; their device ticks perform the actual assertions.

`machine_run` continues advancing device-tick schedules while single-stepping, explicitly using a decrement of one. Therefore a function named `DEVICE_TICK` is not necessarily a SIGALRM callback. [machine.c, machine_run:726](C:/DocumentNoSnc/CC/GXEMUL/GXEMUL-SEC/src/machines/machine.c:726)
