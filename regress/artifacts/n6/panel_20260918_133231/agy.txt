#### Premise P3: Dispositions and Semantic Partitioning
- **ACCEPT at `+0x188` (`IRQ_ENABLE_SET` read):** **CONFIRMED.**
  Per DC21285 Table 7-10 ([`dc21285.txt:6826`](file:///C:/DocumentNoSnc/CC/GXEMUL/_scratchpad/dc21285.txt#L6826)) and Section 7.3.32 ([`dc21285.txt:6911-6925`](file:///C:/DocumentNoSnc/CC/GXEMUL/_scratchpad/dc21285.txt#L6911-L6925)), offset `0x188` is `IRQEnable` on read and `IRQEnableSet` on write. Reading it is a documented, legal operation.
  In [`dev_footbridge.c:586`](file:///C:/DocumentNoSnc/CC/GXEMUL/GXEMUL-SEC/src/devices/dev_footbridge.c#L586), the model already assigns `odata = d->irq_enable;` and immediately calls `fatal()` + `exit(1)`. Deleting `fatal()` and `exit(1)` while returning `odata` (ACCEPT) without a latch or warning is correct.
- **Partitioning of `+0x18c`:** **SPLIT REQUIRED.**
  `+0x18c` cannot share a round group with the status-register writes:
  1. `+0x180`, `+0x184`, `+0x280`, `+0x284` are **writes to read-only registers**.
  2. `+0x18c` is a **read of a write-only register** (`IRQEnableClear`).
  3. `+0x18c` has a critical data leakage bug: line 601 assigns `odata = d->irq_enable;` before exiting. Simply de-fatalizing with decline must **suppress** `d->irq_enable` (returning 0 / undefined), or it leaks internal state. This requires distinct logic and a dedicated oracle, justifying its own sub-group.
- **SDRAM Mask/Offset (`+0x100`, `+0x104`):** **CONFIRMED.**
  Per DC21285 Sections 7.3.9 and 7.3.10 ([`dc21285.txt:5844, 5897`](file:///C:/DocumentNoSnc/CC/GXEMUL/_scratchpad/dc21285.txt#L5844)), both registers are legal R/W. The current model accepts writes but kills on reads ([`dev_footbridge.c:712, 737`](file:///C:/DocumentNoSnc/CC/GXEMUL/GXEMUL-SEC/src/devices/dev_footbridge.c#L712)). Describing a read as "invalid access" would be false in product code; it is an "unimplemented read".

- **Accept Oracle (OR-accumulation):** **CONFIRMED.**
  In [`dev_footbridge.c:580`](file:///C:/DocumentNoSnc/CC/GXEMUL/GXEMUL-SEC/src/devices/dev_footbridge.c#L580), writes accumulate with `d->irq_enable |= idata`. An accept row that writes $V$ then reads back $V$ is indistinguishable from plain RAM. An oracle writing $V_1$ then $V_2$ and asserting $V_1 \mid V_2$ verifies the hardware handler's distinct accumulation logic.
  *Structural Caveat:* The accept sequence must run in its own probe arm (or dedicated phase), **not** in the common `BODY` ([`footbridge_sites_probe.py:92-101`](file:///C:/DocumentNoSnc/CC/GXEMUL/GXEMUL-SEC/regress/footbridge_sites_probe.py#L92-L101)), otherwise arm L dies on the pre-fix build and breaks the liveness controls.
