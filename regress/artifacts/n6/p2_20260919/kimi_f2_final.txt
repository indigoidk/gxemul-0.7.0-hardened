  ## Q3 — what all nine of us missed

  1. **The reap poll's error exit falls *into* the SIGKILL branch** — identical shape in all three probes: `except OSError: break` leaves `pid == 0`, and `if pid == 0:` then sends SIGKILL. `waitpid` raising ECHILD means "no such child" — the one state where the PID is free to be recycled and killing it is certainly wrong. Unreachable today (one reaper, one `close()` per session, no SIGCHLD handler), so latent, not measured; but the guard should be a `gone`/`reaped` flag, not `pid == 0`. Nine seats reviewed poll→kill→wait; all nine reviewed only the happy path.
  2. **The verdict-bearing clock is the drain's patience, and its expiry manufactures a false red on a healthy run under load.** `rd()` returns True on timeout and False only on EOF/EIO (verified in both the Session method and the sites' nested `rd`), so the common case is fine. But a healthy child that needs > 5 s to die after quit (loaded CI host, gate plus sibling probes competing) hits the drain deadline; `os.close(fd)` then drops the master under a *live* child, SIGHUP kills it (default disposition — verified no handler), and the bounded reap honestly reports `exit=None signal=1` → `survived()`/I1/E4/R0 red on a healthy base. To the particular question: **the reap itself cannot misreport a healthy run** — once EOF is seen the child is dead and the first WNOHANG poll reaps it, SIGKILL unreachable; the wrong-verdict state is upstream, in close-before-death, and pass 2 narrowed it (0.3 s fixed → 5 s patience) without eliminating it, while making it verdict-bearing by binding the exit status. Fail-loud, never false-pass; the closure is "drain-deadline expiry ⇒ INCONCLUSIVE, not scored", or a retry-quit before close. This is the healthy-side dual of the filed `fbptymock` leg — the quit-ignoring child and the slow-to-die child are the same unexercised path from opposite ends.
  3. **The reap's final `waitpid(pid, 0)` is still unbounded**, and SIGKILL does not help a child in uninterruptible sleep (D state — plausible under the gate's shared log directory on a loaded disk). Rare and inherited, but "bounded reap" is bounded only against killable states; one contract clause would keep the wording honest.
