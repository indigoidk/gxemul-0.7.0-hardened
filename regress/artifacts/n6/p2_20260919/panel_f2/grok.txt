## Q3 — what did all nine miss?

**1. Bit 31 on the diagnostic channel (L4 in the contract).**  
F12 disclosed bit 31 untested *by value*. The contract still claims a diagnostic kept for bits 30/31 is caught by E4/E2. Q has bits 29 and 30 only. Mutant: SET-read `if (d->irq_enable & 0x80000000) fatal(...)` with or without `exit`. Arm E never sets bit 31 → no diagnostic, values match, K unaffected → **19/19**. Clause (e), not (d). Drop “31” from that sentence.

**2. Q3 as asked — yes, a healthy-run false-fail exists, and it is not the SIGKILL path.**  
`rd()` returns True on select timeout, False on EOF. The drain therefore waits a **5 s wall-clock** after `quit`, not 0.1 s of silence (glm’s pass-2 polarity was inverted). Then the master closes.

GXemul installs no SIGHUP handler, so close-on-a-live-child **terminates it**. `survived()` / I1 / E4 now require `exit == 0`. A healthy child that has not finished `quit` within 5 s dies SIGHUP (`exit=None`, `signal=1`) and those rows red. That is a load-shaped wall-clock oracle of the same family as the old inactivity bug, aimed at debugger quit rather than a boot.

SIGKILL is the *second* stage (another 5 s of `WNOHANG`). On this binary it should almost never run: SIGHUP already killed the child. SIGKILL of a healthy run needs SIGHUP ignored, blocked, or undelivered. Table 2’s `y_sigkill` proves the reap reports signal 9 rather than hanging; it does not prove a healthy quit under battery load stays inside 5 s.

Not a stop: quit at a prompt is milliseconds when the machine is idle, and `TABLE2.log` `base_v2` is 19/19 with D/X/T/E/A exit=0. Record the residual. Do not widen the bound without a measured load fail.

**3. Drain/witness T rows, dropped from the F-list.**  
Codex dedicated named it; the packet did not promote it. After F1 added `survived(T)`, halt is no longer an allowed T outcome, but the predicate is still `rT.get(6) != VENDOR` — `None != 0x1011` is True. A reg-parse miss greens T. Sites C4 documents the inequality as allowing halt; that licence does not transfer once `survived(T)` forbids halt. Require the keys present (`6 in rT and 7 in rT and ...`).
