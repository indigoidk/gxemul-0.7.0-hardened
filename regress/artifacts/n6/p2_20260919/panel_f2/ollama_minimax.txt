## Q3 — What did all 9 of us miss?

**The bounded reap can produce a wrong verdict on a HEALTHY run under load. This is the main miss.**

Walk-through (REASONED):

1. WNOHANG poll for 5 s.
2. If child still alive at 5 s, SIGKILL.
3. Blocking wait returns "killed by signal 9", not a natural exit status.

A healthy child doing legitimate cleanup (log flush, mmaps unmapped, child-of-child reaping, slow I/O) can exceed 5 s under load. The bounded reap then SIGKILLs it. The wait returns `exit=None, signal=9`.

The K row's predicate now sees an outcome it did not expect: neither "exited with status 1" nor "running". Two failure modes follow:

- If the predicate treats any non-zero/non-natural as "test failed" → **false positive**: healthy run reds K.
- If the predicate treats signal-killed as "test not run" → **silent miss**: K does not red; the test was never actually executed; the probe passes spuriously.

Either is a wrong verdict on a healthy run. None of the seats flagged this, because:

- Table 2's x_exit2 / y_sigkill were run on the operator's machine under no artificial load — they do not exercise the timeout path.
- F14's "delayed shutdown" and "ignored quit" mocks are filed, not built — and even when built they will exercise the timeout against an *unhealthy* one, not against a *healthy-but-slow* one.
- The 5 s budget is short enough that this scenario is plausible on a loaded CI box, an overcommitted container, or any host with cold caches.

The fix is one of: (a) raise the initial wait (e.g., 30 s) before the SIGKILL escalation; (b) have the K-row predicate specifically recognise "killed by reap timeout" as a third class distinct from "exited with status X" and "still running", and route it to a separate diagnostic; (c) measure the healthy-run wall time and pick a budget that exceeds it by a safety margin. The bounded-reap shape is correct; the constant is not yet justified by measurement.

**Other things missed (smaller, but worth recording):**

- The Opus 5 measurement (F22) is the only independent kill-table confirmation this round. Without it, the round's confirmation rests on the same main loop that wrote the rows — the brief acknowledges this. fable5 still pending.
- (q) probe-side weakening (F15) is correctly disclosed but not addressed by any new row. The free-running arm is filed. (q) remains an escape class.
- F14's "exit 1 without complaint" failure mode depends on whether K rows check both complaint text AND exit status, or just one. The mock will tell us; until it is built, this case is open.
- Table 2's restoration is "every restore cmp'd CLEAN against SEC and rebuilt fresh" — but the brief does not say how many runs, on what hosts, or whether the cold-cache case was exercised. Restoration correctness under cold cache is its own untested surface.
- D3's (r) is settled by arithmetic (`if (len == 4)` leaves odata 0 → E3 reds). The brief invites an overturn if a measurement is owed. I would not overturn — the arithmetic is short and the construction is direct — but a one-row measurement would be cheap insurance and would also exercise the E6 line, which currently rests on the same arithmetic.
