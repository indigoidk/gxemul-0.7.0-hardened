1. **The decline CHANNEL on Alpha is "destination register UNCHANGED", never zero** (grok, codex; VERIFIED by

   the source register is unchanged on success too); the store-decline channel is the DOWNSTREAM STATE read

2. **`return 0` here is a DELIBERATE DEPARTURE from the file's siblings** (`lca_ioc`'s #518 default arm and
