3. **1 KB Boundary Split and Head/Tail Geometry**:
   * [`src/cpus/memory_rw.c:104, 186-224`](file:///C:/DocumentNoSnc/CC/GXEMUL/GXEMUL-SEC/src/cpus/memory_rw.c#L104): `#define MRW_SPLIT_MASK 0x3ff` (1024-byte granule). For non-PHYSICAL translated accesses, memory accesses straddling a 1 KB boundary are split into a head chunk ending at `1024*k - 1` and a tail chunk starting at `1024*k`.
   * In LCA sparse I/O address space, relative byte address $A$ maps to port $P = A \gg 5$.
   * A 1 KB boundary corresponds to $1024 \gg 5 = 32 = 0\text{x}20$ ports.
   * Any head of length $h \in [1, 7]$ bytes ends at address $1024\cdot k - 1$. Its starting address is $1024\cdot k - h$. Because $h < 32$, $(1024\cdot k - h) \gg 5 = 32\cdot k - 1 \equiv 0\text{x}1\text{f} \pmod{0\text{x}20}$.
   * Since $31 \ \& \ 3 = 3$, **all heads land at lane 3**.
   * Any tail chunk starts at $1024\cdot k$. $(1024\cdot k) \gg 5 = 32\cdot k \equiv 0 \pmod{0\text{x}20}$.
   * Since $0 \ \& \ 3 = 0$, **all tails land at lane 0**.

### Question 2: Can each new row's channel vary under the mutant it names?

**YES. Every discriminator channel was verified in source to respond to its designated mutant and remain stable on base:**

1. **8042 PC-Style Stub (`stub0`-`stub3`)**:
   * [`src/devices/dev_pckbc.c:941-955`](file:///C:/DocumentNoSnc/CC/GXEMUL/GXEMUL-SEC/src/devices/dev_pckbc.c#L941-L955): For `relative_addr != 0 && relative_addr != 4`, `static int x = 0; x++; if (x & 1) odata ^= 0x10;` executes on **both** reads and writes.
   * Two consecutive flat reads bump $x$ twice, toggling bit 4 and then toggling it back, leaving the difference between successive parity checks zero.
   * Under a single access, bit 4 changes state once. Bracketing an LCA access between flat reads produces 2 total accesses on base (unchanged parity, $\Delta \ \& \ 0\text{x}10 == 0$).
   * Under a phantom read or duplicated read, 3 total accesses occur (parity flips, $\Delta \ \& \ 0\text{x}10 == 0\text{x}10 \ne 0$), turning the row RED.
   * Liveness rows (`stub%d live`) perform two plain flat reads without an intervening LCA access; they expect a flip of $0\text{x}10$. If the stub is ever implemented or removed, the liveness row fails immediately.
