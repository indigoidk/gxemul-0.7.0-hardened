### 2. Answers to Section 3 Questions

#### Q1. Shape of `[NOT A PANEL STAGE: NO PANEL CONVENED]` vs Two Specific Tokens
The generic shape `[NOT A PANEL STAGE: NO PANEL CONVENED]` is **structurally flawed** because it removes the checkable claim from the token literal and buries it into unparsed freeform prose in `note`:
1. Neither [`check_stage_panels.py:165-167`](file:///C:/DocumentNoSnc/CC/GXEMUL/GXEMUL-SEC/tools/pipeline/check_stage_panels.py#L165-L167) nor [`check_fable_queue.py:115-117`](file:///C:/DocumentNoSnc/CC/GXEMUL/GXEMUL-SEC/tools/pipeline/check_fable_queue.py#L115-L117) inspects or validates the note; any string attached to `NOPANEL` unconditionally silences both gates.
2. It conflates two distinct operational regimes:
   - **Incidental / Misdirected Reviews**: Reviews exist on disk, but the panel was not convened on this row (e.g. `asiceventaddr`, `pcc2c040assertrow`).
   - **Non-Panel Engineering Records**: Work was done directly (e.g. `ip30abortkill assess` rung-3 witness build, `lcalenzerodrop assess` kill table measurements, `landiskaux assess` main-loop verification).
3. Two distinct tokens—e.g. `[NOT A PANEL STAGE: INCIDENTAL MENTION]` and `[NOT A PANEL STAGE: AUTHOR RECORD]`—would preserve semantic auditability and prevent the token from becoming a universal blanket bypass.

#### Q2. Bypassing a Short Stage: Concrete Misuse & Checker Blindness
* **Concrete Misuse:** A round executes a panel where 6 of 9 seats answer, but 3 seats time out or fail on quota. Under deadline or quota exhaustion, an operator adds:
  ```json
  {"seat": "fable5", "phase": "review", "date": "2026-09-18", "note": "[NOT A PANEL STAGE: NO PANEL CONVENED] Quota exhausted; waiving remaining seats"}
  ```
* **Can Any Check Catch It?** **NO.**
  - [`check_stage_panels.py:217-222`](file:///C:/DocumentNoSnc/CC/GXEMUL/GXEMUL-SEC/tools/pipeline/check_stage_panels.py#L217-L222): Setting `_annotated = True` immediately routes the stage to `grandfathered += 1` and skips seat-count enforcement.
  - [`check_fable_queue.py:129-130`](file:///C:/DocumentNoSnc/CC/GXEMUL/GXEMUL-SEC/tools/pipeline/check_fable_queue.py#L129-L130): `d["annotated"]` causes the checker to skip the stage.
  - [`check_seats_read.py:23-28`](file:///C:/DocumentNoSnc/CC/GXEMUL/GXEMUL-SEC/tools/pipeline/check_seats_read.py#L23-L28): Passes because all 6 seats in the cited panel directory are recorded.
  - Unlike `HELD`, which has an anti-loophole that fails if the row is closed ([`check_stage_panels.py:200-202`](file:///C:/DocumentNoSnc/CC/GXEMUL/GXEMUL-SEC/tools/pipeline/check_stage_panels.py#L200-L202)), `NOPANEL` has **no anti-loophole** on closed rows (as seen on `unreadseats23` and `pcc2c040assertrow`).
  - The discriminator ("read `ev` and check if `brief_sent.md` named this row") is written as prose instruction in comments ([`check_stage_panels.py:102-106`](file:///C:/DocumentNoSnc/CC/GXEMUL/GXEMUL-SEC/tools/pipeline/check_stage_panels.py#L102-L106)), but **no code implements it**.

#### Q3. `gen_dashboard.py` Dropping Annotated Entries to Blank (`·`)
* **Status:** **YES, IT IS A NEW CONFLATION.**
* In [`gen_dashboard.py:116-117`](file:///C:/DocumentNoSnc/CC/GXEMUL/GXEMUL-SEC/tools/pipeline/gen_dashboard.py#L116-L117), filtering out annotated entries causes `cell()` to return `<td class="c none"><span class="g">·</span></td>`.
* The dashboard legend ([`gen_dashboard.py:369`](file:///C:/DocumentNoSnc/CC/GXEMUL/GXEMUL-SEC/tools/pipeline/gen_dashboard.py#L369)) explicitly defines `·` as `no input — input is possible`.
* For a stage where no panel was convened (or a closed row with `NOPANEL`), input is **not** possible or expected.
* On rows with partial answers plus `NOPANEL` (such as `pcc2c040assertrow` with 8 seats or `armmsrmodekill` with 4 seats), answered seats render with checkmarks while the remaining seats render as `·` (`input is possible`), actively misleading readers into believing a live 9-seat panel is simply awaiting the missing seats.
* To satisfy the core invariant that "different states must not render the same" ([`check_stage_panels.py:158-159`](file:///C:/DocumentNoSnc/CC/GXEMUL/GXEMUL-SEC/tools/pipeline/check_stage_panels.py#L158-L159)), annotated non-panel stages should render a distinct glyph (e.g. `—` or `N/A` with a descriptive tooltip), not the input-possible middle dot `·`.
