**Additional finding — H2's guarantee is weaker than stated (READ from §6):** `literal_offenders` walks `ast.Constant` nodes. The parser merges *implicitly* concatenated literals (`"[NOT A " "SEAT ANSWER]"` → one constant, caught), but explicit folding is invisible: `"[NOT A SEAT " + "ANSWER]"`, `JoinredStr` halves, `.format` assembly — none contain a full token under the bar, so the "no token literal elsewhere" tripwire is defeatable. The *accidental* case (line-wrapped implicit concat) is caught; only deliberate assembly evades. Fix is one constant-folding pass over BinOp/JoinedStr, or downgrade the claim in the precommit comment. Minor; file, don't block.
