## 4. The note in the key

Keep it.

A count of `(row, phase, seat, date, d)` cannot tell a note correction from a conversion plus a new answer. Those two ledgers are different if the checker also looks at glyph siblings: a pure note edit leaves one entry, and the measured refill leaves the frozen note on a `✓` beside the new answer. Pairing a glyph only when its note hash is the frozen one rejects that refill and allows the pure note edit.

That rule still has a bypass: convert the entry, change its note as well, and add the new off-domain answer. The frozen note is then gone, and the new answer looks like a note edit. Treating any same-date glyph as the consumer of the allowance closes the bypass and false-fails real rows. kimi's review dated 2026-08-25 carries both `d: "✗"` and `d: "✓"` with different notes. A note correction of the `✗` would be refused because the `✓` is already there.

The note hash is the rule that closes the refill without depending on that sibling shape. The price is C16: to keep a corrected note, give that entry a glyph or omit `d`. Say that as this check's toll. It is not forced by the ruling.
