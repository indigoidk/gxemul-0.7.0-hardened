**DEFECT 1 — PRETABLE §1 mis-attributes what `two declined` pins.** The brief states "the `two declined' row is what pins 'skipped' rather than 'returned early' for the second PUT." This is wrong. Walk the row:

- Fix (`ok = 0` + `if (ok)` guard): second PUT sets `ok = 0`, guard skips `memory_rw`, destination = paint. Row expects paint. **Pass.**
- Early `return` placed AFTER the latch block (announce + latch still fire, then return): second PUT finds latch set, no announce, returns before the store, destination = paint. Row expects paint. **Pass.**

The two mutants are observationally indistinguishable on this row. `two declined` catches `M4_okinlatch` (where the second PUT's `ok` is left at 1 and the store runs with an unassigned `buf`), but it does NOT catch early return.

**DEFECT 2 — Q2 falls out of Defect 1.** The smallest edit that violates the design's "never an early `return`: the common tail is that store" rule and still passes all 14 rows is to move `return;` to after the latch block in the default arm (and drop the now-dead `ok = 0;`). All 14 rows stay green. No row reddens. The PRETABLE's claim that `two declined` would redden it is the trap; it does not.
