## One defect in the brief's own explanation

The brief says "`$(...)` strips the list's last newline and `while read` then DROPS THE LAST FILE". The `$(...)` is not what strips here — the bug is that bash's `read` returns non-zero on a partial final line (no `\n` before EOF), so the loop body never runs for it. The fix (`printf '%s\n'`) is correct regardless.
