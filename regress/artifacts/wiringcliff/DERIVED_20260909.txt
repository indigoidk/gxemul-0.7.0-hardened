6. *** MY OWN SECTION-2 CLAIM IS TOO BROAD, AND THE codex SEAT CORRECTED IT.
   codex_contracts_20260909.txt/.err, fired 2026-09-09 with the premise stated AS A QUESTION. ***
================================================================================
Section 2 says the four WDC harnesses have "NO nonzero exit path".  codex checked it and refined
it, which is what stating a premise as a question is FOR:

  "Premise 2 is correct about the absence of `sys.exit(', but 'cannot exit nonzero' is too broad.
   The main altoffset file has two uncaught parent-process assertions: label width and distinct
   result registers ... Both companion altoffset files execute those assertions through their
   import.  Missing arguments or malformed leg specifications can also raise exceptions in the
   companions; uncaught filesystem and PTY errors can terminate all four."

  "Neither PTY implementation propagates the child's exit status.  Failed row comparisons and
   unsuccessful breakpoint observations have no explicit nonzero exit path. ... Thus your
   conclusion holds for an exit-status-only gate evaluating completed measurements."

**THE CORRECT STATEMENT, and the distinction is load-bearing:** these files CAN exit nonzero -- on
an uncaught exception (a bad argument, a missing file, a PTY error, a violated table-integrity
assert).  What they cannot do is exit nonzero because a MEASUREMENT FAILED.  So the gate-section
consequence is unchanged -- a wired section still cannot fail on the thing it exists to test --
but the sentence "no nonzero exit path" is false as written and would be reused.  *An APPARATUS
failure and a MEASUREMENT failure must not render the same, which is this project's oldest rule
about blanks arriving at exit statuses.*
