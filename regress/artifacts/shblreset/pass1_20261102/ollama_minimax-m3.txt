**REASONED — (d) Ordinary interrupts are gated, but the “would be rewritten too” sentence is wrong.** Under the packet’s settled premise that the quoted dynamic-translation gate is the ordinary interrupt route and NMI is absent, ordinary interrupts do not reach the BL block.
