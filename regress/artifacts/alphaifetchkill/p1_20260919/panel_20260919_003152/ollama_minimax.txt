**The defect in option (e):**

The brief itself states (emphasis mine): *"It is a design precedent, **not a drop-in**: the run entry resets instruction accounting after PC conversion (:267), and the epilogue reconstructs the PC from instruction pointers (:267, :394)."*

Option (e) as listed ("next_ic -> nothing_call, debugger_reset()") does not address any of these three concerns:

1. **`cur_ic_page`:** The brief correctly notes option (b) is refuted because it "skips setting `cur_ic_page`/`next_ic` (:830-833)." Option (e) only addresses `next_ic`. It does not state what `cur_ic_page` holds on return, nor whether the dispatch loop touches it after `pc_to_pointers` returns. If `cur_ic_page` is stale/NULL, the dispatch loop may still crash. The brief does not distinguish why (e) survives this where (b) does not.

2. **Instruction-accounting reset at `:267`:** The brief says this runs "after PC conversion." If `pc_to_pointers` returned without converting, what does the reset do with an unconverted PC? Crash? Corrupt count? Silently wrong? Not addressed.

3. **PC reconstruction at `:394`:** If `next_ic` points to `nothing_call`, the instruction pointers fed to this reconstruction are the `nothing_call` template, not the faulting VA. The witness asks (Q3) that `pc` still equal the faulting VA -- option (e) provides no mechanism to preserve `cpu->pc`. It sets `running = 0` but never snapshots the faulting PC before the run loop's epilogue overwrites it via reconstruction.

**Other gaps:**

- **`nothing_call` definition and safety:** Not inlined. Whether it is a pure nop, whether it touches `cur_ic_page`, or whether it expects `ic_page` state is unknown from the packet.
- **Q1 sub-question "single-step trace read (:287, :325) really handle its own failure":** Lines referenced but not quoted. Cannot settle.
- **Q3 detector rows:** The brief asks which rows prove "host survives AND CPU stopped AND no garbage executes." For "no garbage executes," option (e) must demonstrate that the instruction *preceding* the failed translation (e.g., the `JMP`) has fully retired and that no instruction at the faulting VA is fetched. The brief does not address instruction retirement boundaries around `pc_to_pointers`.
- **`-V` debugger prompt:** Mechanism unexplained. `debugger_reset()` + `running = 0` does not, by themselves in the inlined code, obviously produce a debugger prompt; this relies on out-of-packet behavior.

**Bottom line:** The defect at `:729-733` is real, and Alpha's failure mode is convincingly witnessed. But the recommended fix (e) is presented as complete while the brief simultaneously identifies three concrete integration hazards it does not resolve. A seat that ratifies (e) on this packet is trusting the author to have worked those out off-page.
