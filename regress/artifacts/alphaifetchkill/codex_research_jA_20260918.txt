VERDICT: e -- Halt the affected CPU and stop dyntrans safely; its dispatch loop never checks `cpu->running`.

Confirmed at `HEAD cdbe7d7`. I read only the five permitted sources. Citations use working-file lines; PALcode has 23 locally added comment lines, with no executable change.

The observed defect is real: Alpha’s `not_found` prints a message and returns zero without updating the PC or supplying a physical address ([memory_alpha.c:192](C:/DocumentNoSnc/CC/GXEMUL/GXEMUL-SEC/src/cpus/memory_alpha.c:192)). Dyntrans assumes exception delivery, reloads `cpu->pc`, retries, and calls `exit(1)` after another failure ([cpu_dyntrans.c:685](C:/DocumentNoSnc/CC/GXEMUL/GXEMUL-SEC/src/cpus/cpu_dyntrans.c:685), [cpu_dyntrans.c:721](C:/DocumentNoSnc/CC/GXEMUL/GXEMUL-SEC/src/cpus/cpu_dyntrans.c:721)). **The host process exits; the guest receives no Alpha fault.**

There are two essential implementation traps:

- **Replacing only `exit(1)` with `running = 0` falls through to `physaddr = paddr`**, although Alpha’s failure supplied no `paddr` ([cpu_dyntrans.c:736](C:/DocumentNoSnc/CC/GXEMUL/GXEMUL-SEC/src/cpus/cpu_dyntrans.c:736)).
- **Adding a bare `return` is also insufficient.** It skips initialization of `cur_ic_page` and `next_ic` ([cpu_dyntrans.c:830](C:/DocumentNoSnc/CC/GXEMUL/GXEMUL-SEC/src/cpus/cpu_dyntrans.c:830)). The caller continues after PC conversion, and instruction dispatch dereferences `next_ic` without checking `running` ([cpu_dyntrans.c:190](C:/DocumentNoSnc/CC/GXEMUL/GXEMUL-SEC/src/cpus/cpu_dyntrans.c:190), [cpu_dyntrans.c:114](C:/DocumentNoSnc/CC/GXEMUL/GXEMUL-SEC/src/cpus/cpu_dyntrans.c:114)).
