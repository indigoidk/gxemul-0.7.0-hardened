For **Q1**, `gdromcondavail` wins on established present harm, not its title. Its wording still needs qualification: **ordinary exhaustion can clear readiness**, and another command can recover the state; the persistent lie concerns repeated reads after entering the inconsistent state. I found no present host termination, reachable OOB access, or established guest-memory corruption among these fifteen.

For **Q2**, the clearest outright non-defects are **`wdcperopcode` and `asicarray4`**. Keep those separate from already repaired findings (`wdcflood`, `pitclobber`, `asiceventaddr`), latent hazards, and detector debt.

Two hardware checks sharpen that answer:

- **`sh4pcic15`:** Renesas defines encoded IRL value 15 as **no interrupt request**, priority zero; registering an unused endpoint does not establish a missing valid hardware interrupt. Conversely, FRQCR’s reset bits 11–9 are all one, confirming that its zero reset report is wrong. [Renesas hardware manual, §§19.2.2 and 10.4.1](https://www.renesas.com/en/document/mah/sh7750-sh7750s-sh7750r-group-users-manual-hardware).
- **`gdromdmawidth`:** the Linux driver really does byte-write and byte-read the DMA status/start register while using full-word writes for the four value registers. That constrains a correction but does **not** establish narrow-write semantics for those value registers. [Linux GD-ROM driver](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/torvalds/linux/master/drivers/cdrom/gdrom.c).

For **Q3, promote `sh4sqwrite` ahead of the latent ASIC proposals.** Its title sounds like missing tests, but [memory_sh.c](C:/DocumentNoSnc/CC/GXEMUL/GXEMUL-SEC/src/cpus/memory_sh.c:352), anchor `Store queue region:`, grants read/write access directly, and [dev_sh4.c](C:/DocumentNoSnc/CC/GXEMUL/GXEMUL-SEC/src/devices/dev_sh4.c:1574), anchor `DEVICE_ACCESS(sh4_sq)`, accepts the writes into queue storage. Hardware requires write-type exception checks on stores into those queues. [Renesas §4.7.4](https://www.renesas.com/en/document/mah/sh7750-sh7750s-sh7750r-group-users-manual-hardware).

That is a **guest-reachable missing protection check**. “No known guest depends on it” does not establish unreachability. Its `none yet` witness still needs attention: [`sq pr00 evt` and `sq d0 evt`](C:/DocumentNoSnc/CC/GXEMUL/GXEMUL-SEC/regress/sh_halt_probe.py:597) exercise PREF’s read-type judgement, so they cannot witness the missing store-side check.

For **Q4**, give **`shprotld`, `asicpermute`, and `asickillsurvive` priority through gate blindness** despite their low current product-harm ratings. Their named probes respectively lack the necessary privilege/access combination, an interrupt-semantic observation, and decline-value/state assertions. `gdromcondavail` also has a stimulus already present without the relevant state oracle.
