# Audit the adjudication packet before the flagship seat reads it tomorrow

**VERDICT LINE FIRST.**

**The tree is `GXEMUL-SEC/src/`** — ignore the pristine 2021 copy at the project root.

**READ THE FILE:** `GXEMUL-SEC/tools/pipeline/ADJUDICATION_20260909.md` (about 34 KB). It is the
packet a flagship seat will adjudicate from tomorrow, when its usage window resets. **It is the
only thing that seat will see.** If a claim in it is wrong, the ruling built on that claim is wrong,
and nothing downstream will catch it.

## What this packet is

It carries rows advanced over the last two days with my disposition on each, plus questions I am
deliberately NOT answering myself. Its opening section **withdraws an earlier guarantee** and
replaces it with five evidentiary categories — MEASURED, SOURCE-DERIVED, SEAT FINDING, PREDICTION,
MY JUDGMENT. That distinction is the packet's load-bearing structure.

## The job

**Find the claims that are doing work they have not earned.** Not style, not tone — claims.

**Q1. CATEGORY DISCIPLINE.** The packet sorts its claims into those five categories. **Where does a
claim sit in a stronger category than its evidence supports?** In particular: a PREDICTION written
in a measured column, or a SEAT FINDING restated as though I had verified it. This project has a
recorded history of exactly that — a table once carried a predicted value in a measured column and
the score matched by coincidence.

**Q2. THE SEVERITIES.** Two rows were raised or referred on my judgment this week, and both say so.
**Is any OTHER severity in the packet effectively mine while being presented as a seat's?**

**Q3. NUMBERS.** Every count in the packet should name its deriver or carry its method. **Which
numbers are bare literals, and of those, which are load-bearing for a disposition?** You do not need
to re-derive them all — name the ones where being wrong would change a decision.

**Q4. THE OMISSION QUESTION, and it is the one I cannot ask myself.** The packet has a section
titled "WHAT I DID NOT DO, deliberately". **What is missing from the packet that is NOT in that
section?** An adjudicator ruling on an incomplete docket rules wrongly and has no way to know.

**Q5. INTERNAL CONSISTENCY.** Sections were appended over two days. **Does any later section
contradict an earlier one, or restate a claim an earlier section already withdrew?** The packet
corrects itself in at least two places; a correction that landed in one section and not another is
the failure mode.

**Answer with a numbered list of specific claims and what is wrong with each.** If a section is
sound, say so briefly rather than padding it. An honest "I found three things and the rest reads
clean" is more useful than a survey.
