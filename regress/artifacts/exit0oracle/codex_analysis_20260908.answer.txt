  The decisive answer to Q2 is that **`exit0` is synthesized from the child’s exit status**. Each eligible file defines its own local `session` helper; none requires `regress/lib.sh`. The [usual helper](C:/DocumentNoSnc/CC/GXEMUL/GXEMUL-SEC/regress/gdrom_toclen_probe.py:135) returns three independent observations:
  
  - The captured PTY transcript.
  - `alive`, which records whether a PTY read or write failure was observed during command processing.
  - `how`, obtained by reaping the child and interpreting its wait status: normal termination becomes `exit` followed by the numeric status; signal termination becomes `sig` followed by the signal number.
  
  The helper attempts debugger shutdown before reaping. **It does not establish that this shutdown caused the termination.** Consequently, an earlier exit zero and an ordinary debugger shutdown can both produce `exit0`. Neither matching output nor PTY EOF produces that token.
  
  Ordinary read-budget expiration also does not establish progress: the read helper can return true without receiving anything. Most copies subsequently use a blocking wait. The [PCC2 detector’s variant](C:/DocumentNoSnc/CC/GXEMUL/GXEMUL-SEC/regress/pcc2_width_probe.py:474) additionally handles breakpoint-token timeouts and bounded reaping, preserving outcomes such as `no-breakpoint`, `killed`, and `unreaped`. It still does not derive `exit0` from evidence that the access completed.
  
  For Q3, therefore, **the probe has enough information to distinguish the outcomes, but a bare `exit0` row does not use it**. An asserted result produced after the relevant access can establish continuation. Merely collecting that result without asserting it cannot. A diagnostic emitted before termination, even matched exactly once, cannot establish continuation either.
  
  There is also a distinction within your concern: continued execution does not itself prove refusal. If the diagnostic remains while the refused operation takes effect, distinguishing that requires an assertion about the refusal’s resulting state or prohibited effects. Conversely, a bare status check remains useful against nonzero exits and signals; it is not literally incapable of failing.
