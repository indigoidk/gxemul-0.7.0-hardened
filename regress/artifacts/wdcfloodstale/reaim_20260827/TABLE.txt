#486 wdcfloodstale -- the re-aimed `overrun x2`, measured on the shipped bytes.

Every number here was produced by the main loop on cf300c0-era HEAD, and every one of
them was ALSO produced independently by a panel seat.  They agree.

  build                                     WDC_FLOOD_RESULT   overrun x2
  ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
  shipped, BEFORE the re-aim                    25/26          0      want 1   RED
  shipped, AFTER  the re-aim                    26/26          1,1,0  want 1,1,0  ok
  Mb2 -- the producer's latch DELETED           25/26          1,145,0            FAIL

*** THE OLD ROW WAS NON-DISCRIMINATING, NOT MERELY RED, AND THAT IS THE FINDING. ***
Measured by a seat on the committed binary: the old stimulus reads OVERRUN = 0 on the
shipped latch AND 0 on a build with the latch deleted.  Clause (b) was OPEN at the ring
producer with nothing anywhere saying so.  The re-aimed row reads 1 against 145 -- it
discriminates by a factor of 145.

*** 145 WAS PREDICTED BEFORE IT WAS MEASURED, THREE TIMES INDEPENDENTLY. ***  My brief
derived it, the timing seat measured it, and I then measured it again here on my own
mutant.  Derivation, so the number carries its deriver: WDC_INBUF_SIZE = 512*513 =
262,656; nothing drains, so tail stays 0; the producer refuses at head == 262,655, so
push 262,656 is the first refusal -- the SECOND push of store 131,328.  At 2 pushes per
mov.w that gives 1 + 2*(131,400 - 131,328) = 145.

*** THE CLIFF, AND IT IS WHY THE COUNT IS NOT 131328. ***  Measured by the timing seat
across four builds:

      n              131328      131329      131400
      shipped           1 ok        1 ok        1 ok
      latch DELETED     1 VACUOUS   3 red     145 red

At the exact fill point the unlatched mutant is GREEN.  "Tidying" 131400 down to the
arithmetically exact 131328 makes the comment MORE precise and silently re-opens the L2
breach.  The margin is a load-bearing product of the row, and the row comment says so.

THE ANTI-FAKE CONTROL IS THE THIRD ORACLE VALUE.  The smallest way to fake this row is to
re-anchor P_OVERRUN onto the `:704` DATA text: every build then reads 1, and `disjoint`
CANNOT catch it because its own stimulus fires neither pattern.  So the row counts both
sites and asserts NO SINGLE LINE is matched by both.  Asserting "1,1" alone does not close
it -- a P_OVERRUN aimed at the DATA line still gives 1,1.

WHY FREE-RUNNING, AND THE STATED REASON IS NOT THE OBVIOUS ONE.  It is not that ~394,000
single-steps are too slow.  `dev_wdc.c` sets `d->data_debug = 1` at attach, so every
data-port write calls `debug()` -- which PRINTS under stepping and is silent free-running
at verbose 0.  Measured at full size by the timing seat:

      step  mov.w x131400   30.684 s   131,400 debug lines   20,369,040 bytes of pty
      fast  mov.w x131400    0.005 s         0 debug lines

So the stepped form WORKS, at 30 s and 20 MB per row.  Free-running is also a CAPABILITY
gain: a `fatal(` -> `debug(` downgrade at this site now REDS, which a stepped row could
not see -- so the probe's own contract sentence about channel downgrades is narrowed.

CAUSE OF DEATH, MEASURED BY SINGLE-VARIABLE CONTROL: revert ONLY #479's unconditional
`wdc_new_command_reset()` and keep #484's ring -> OVERRUN goes 0 -> 1.  #479 is the
killer.  The header had blamed a FUTURE ring fix; #484's repair changes the number of
overrun EVENTS, not the LATCHED count this row asserts, so it would not have moved the
answer at all.

RECORDS CORRECTED IN THE SAME DIFF, because the re-aim falsified them:
  * the header's re-pin trigger, which named the wrong round;
  * `wr indep`'s census -- "Zero rows fire two sites" and "`overrun x2` uses legal 8-bit
    writes" are both now false, deliberately: firing both sites is what kills the
    flag-pairing mutant.

`spin=25.0` removed: free-running finishes in ~0.02 s, so the gate reclaims ~25 s a run.
