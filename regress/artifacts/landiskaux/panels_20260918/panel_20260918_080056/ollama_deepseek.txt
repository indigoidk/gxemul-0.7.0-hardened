**Q1 — Multiplier form vs literal**  
READ: The existing three cases use bare offsets (`+0x160`, `+0x402`) or an absolute (`0x14000386`), none multiply by `addr_mult`.  
REASONED: The packet gives no evidence that the alt-status offset scales with `addr_mult`. `addr_mult=2` for landisk today, so both forms produce `0x1400002c`, but the multiplier form over-generalizes from one data point. The house idiom is a bare byte offset. **Prefer `alt_status_addr = devinit->addr + 0x2c;`**, not `0x16 * devinit->addr_mult`.

**Q2 — Where does 0x16 come from?**  
READ: The brief states measured guest writes at `0x1400002c` and `addr_mult=2`; arithmetic gives `0x2c / 2 = 0x16`.  
REASONED: That is not independent evidence — it is the same fact expressed in different units. The packet contains no SH4/landisk source that states the Device Control / Alt Status offset in register units. **Single fact that would settle it:** a hardware or driver specification giving the offset as a register index (e.g., “alt status is at register 0x16”) or explicitly as a byte offset (e.g., “offset 0x2c from base”).
