VERDICT: CONFIRMED, AND THE PREVIOUS SEAT'S NUMBERS WERE RIGHT.  OpenBSD 7.6
issues exactly TWELVE byte writes to paddr 0x1400002c and ZERO reads, reproduced
on four independent runs.  Six carry 0x08 (WDCTL_4BIT), five carry 0x02
(WDCTL_IDS), and the FIRST carries 0x0c = WDCTL_RST | WDCTL_4BIT -- so a real ATA
SOFT RESET is among them and is silently discarded today.  The command block at
0x14000000..0x1400000f SUCCEEDS: 12,718 accesses across all eight registers, and
wd0 attaches with correct geometry, so devinit->addr is RIGHT and the defect is a
missing offset case, not a wrong base.  Nothing else in the constructed machine
claims 0x1400002c; the registered wdc_altstatus window at 0x14000206 receives
ZERO accesses for the entire boot.

  *** THE WATCHPOINT RUN SAW 9 OF THE 12, AND THAT IS A PROPERTY OF THE METHOD,
  NOT A DISAGREEMENT.  The check in MEMORY_RW_ONE_PAGE is guarded by
  !single_step && !about_to_enter_single_step, and the hit itself SETS
  about_to_enter_single_step -- so a write arriving before the debugger has been
  entered and resumed is not reported.  It is a LOWER BOUND by construction.  It
  confirms the VALUES; the COUNT of 12 rests on the other two instruments. ***

WHAT MAPPING THE REGISTER WOULD THEN DO (read from DEVICE_ACCESS(wdc_altstatus)):
  * on the 0x0c write, idata & WDCTL_RST is true, so the handler sets
    d->cur_command = COMMAND_RESET and calls wdc_new_command_reset(), which
    clears atapi_cmd_in_progress, atapi_phase and write_in_progress and purges
    the inbuf ring (inbuf_head = inbuf_tail = 0).  The guard is LEVEL-triggered
    by deliberate #481 design.
  * the six 0x08 and five 0x02 writes reach no WDCTL_RST branch; they would
    produce only a debug() line, invisible without -v.
  * reads would return status_byte(), which is PURE (verified by reading it: it
    derives from diskimage_exist, the ring pointers, write_in_progress, d->error
    and the atapi phase, and assigns nothing).  Today they return 0 instead --
    but the guest performs ZERO reads there in this boot, so that half is not
    observable on this rig.
  * cur_command = COMMAND_RESET (0x100) is a LATCH.  Its only two consumers are
    the cyl_lo and cyl_hi read arms, and BOTH are gated on
    diskimage_is_a_cdrom(), so on this IDE-disk rig they do not fire.

  NOT SETTLED: whether delivering the reset changes the boot outcome, and what a
  CD-ROM rig (-d ic:) would do -- both consumers of the latch are cdrom-gated and
  this census used a plain IDE disk.  Those are fix-side measurements the round
  still owes; this census does not answer them.

================================================================================
A TRAP WORTH RECORDING -- IT PRODUCED A CLEAN, CONFIDENT, HALF-EMPTY CENSUS
================================================================================
The first version of the census hook tested its window on paddr at do_return_ok.
It reported 12 unmapped writes and ZERO command-block accesses, with no error and
no missing denominator -- and that was wrong.  The device arm of
MEMORY_RW_ONE_PAGE does "paddr -= mem->devices[i].baseaddr" BEFORE calling the
handler, so a device access reaches do_return_ok carrying the RELATIVE offset
(0x00..0x0f), never 0x14000000+.  A window predicate written on paddr is
therefore blind to exactly the mapped half -- which is the half that answers Q3.
The fix is the GXC_P0 latch, taken immediately after address translation.

*** THIS IS PRECISELY HOW A REPORT COULD COME BACK WITH THE UNMAPPED WRITES AND
NO COMMAND-BLOCK CONTROL AT ALL.  A censusing instrument must be shown to see the
POSITIVE case before its negatives are believed; here the 2560/2560 DATA
agreement against the tree's own -v output is that proof. ***
