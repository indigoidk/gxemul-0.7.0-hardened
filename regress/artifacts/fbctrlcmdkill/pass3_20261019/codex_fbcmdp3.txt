1. **The enumeration is incomplete.**

   I checked the producer before enumerating the domain. `memory_readmax64()` assembles unsigned bytes into a zero-initialized `uint64_t`: the scalar write domains are `0..255`, `0..65535`, `0..2^32−1`, and `0..2^64−1`. Narrow stores do not produce sign-extended wide aliases. MIPS unaligned stores use whole 4- or 8-byte callbacks, so they do not introduce lengths 3, 5, 6, or 7. The 16-byte case is already disclosed. See [memory.c](/C:/DocumentNoSnc/cc/GXEMUL/GXEMUL-SEC/src/core/memory.c:55) and [the unaligned producer](/C:/DocumentNoSnc/cc/GXEMUL/GXEMUL-SEC/src/cpus/cpu_mips_instr_unaligned.c:131).

   The advertised **16 bound-edge cells are all reached**: `{accept 2, refuse 3} × {latch clear, latch set} × {1,2,4,8 bytes}`. That arithmetic is correct. Reaching those cells does not establish every promised effect.

   The actual read coverage is:

   | Port partition | Incoming latch | Widths reached | Missing widths |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | COMMAND | Clear | 1, 2, 4, 8 | None |
   | COMMAND | Set | 4 | 1, 2, 8 |
   | Non-COMMAND | Clear | 4 | 1, 2, 8 |
   | Non-COMMAND | Set | 4 | 1, 2, 8 |

   Thus **7 of 16** port × latch × read-width cells are reached. Non-COMMAND writes reach all four widths with the latch clear, but only width 4 with it set: **5 of 8** cells.

   More decisively, persistence has only **2 of 8 observed cells**: `{accept, read} × {1,2,4,8}`. T2 witnesses persistence through `sw` NOP and `lw`; W:latch has no refusal after its accepted commands. [Frozen sessions](/C:/DocumentNoSnc/cc/GXEMUL/GXEMUL-SEC/regress/artifacts/fbctrlcmdkill/pass3_20261019/frozen/fbctrl_cmd_probe.v4.py.txt:437).

   Concrete escaping edits:

   | Missing obligation | Exact edit | Rows expected to remain green |

6. **We missed the difference between visiting a cell and observing its effects before they disappear.**

   Two additional examples make that concrete:

   - At refusal entry, add `if (len == 1 && d->warned_cmd) d->port[d->current_port] = idata;`. W:latch’s byte refusal corrupts COMMAND, but its later GET overwrites the corruption before `wl cmd`.
   - Guard the accepted store with `if (!(len == 2 && d->warned_cmd))`, or the analogous width-8 condition. W:latch already holds COMMAND=2 at those GETs, so skipping their stores preserves both dispatch effects and final readback.

   Both predict all 123 rows green. The repair needs an observation matrix alongside the reachability matrix: grade refusal effects before later commands overwrite them, use distinguishable prior COMMAND values for store witnesses, and place a further refusal after each accept/read used to claim latch persistence. Adding R:edges’ already-collected X1/Y1 assertions closes the strongest no-width escape immediately; completing the width obligation requires the remaining cells above.
