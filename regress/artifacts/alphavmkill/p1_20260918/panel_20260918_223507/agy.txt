### 2. Adversarial Review of Section 5 Questions

#### Q1 -- Premises and Address Arithmetic
1. **Is `0xfffffffd80000000` special to NetBSD/alpha?**
   **Yes.** NetBSD/alpha configures an 8 GB self-mapped Virtual Page Table (VPT) at `0xfffffffc00000000` (level-1 index `i1 = 0x3fe`, `i2 = 0x0`, `i3 = 0x0`). 
   Address `0xfffffffd80000000` has indices `i1 = 0x3fe`, `i2 = 0x300` (768), `i3 = 0x0`, placing it at offset exactly **6.0 GB** into the 8 GB VPT. 
   In 3-level Alpha paging with 8 KB pages and 8-byte PTEs, offset 6 GB in the VPT indexes the PTE for virtual address:
   $$\frac{6 \times 1024^3 \text{ bytes}}{8 \text{ bytes/PTE}} \times 8192 \text{ bytes/page} = 6 \text{ TB} = \text{0x0000060000000000}$$
   Sign-extending bit 42 gives `0xfffffe0000000000`, which is the exact base of **K1SEG** (Alpha kernel virtual memory).
   Anders Gavare encountered this address during early NetBSD boot when the kernel accessed the page table entry for the beginning of K1SEG, prompting the message `"Alpha kernel virtual memory seems to want to be used"`.
2. **Member 4 (ACCEPT) Failure Mode**:
   Under Member 4, [`memory_alpha.c:74`](file:///C:/DocumentNoSnc/CC/GXEMUL/GXEMUL-SEC/src/cpus/memory_alpha.c#L74) executes:
   `*return_paddr = 0xfffffffd80000000ULL & 0x000003ffffffffffULL = 0x000003fd80000000ULL` (~4 TB physical).
   In [`memory_rw.c:663-714`](file:///C:/DocumentNoSnc/CC/GXEMUL/GXEMUL-SEC/src/cpus/memory_rw.c#L663-L714), this address is well past `mem->physical_max` (64 MB) and no device matches. 
   - On **READ**: [`memory_rw.c:683`](file:///C:/DocumentNoSnc/CC/GXEMUL/GXEMUL-SEC/src/cpus/memory_rw.c#L683) zeroes the buffer and jumps to [`do_return_ok`](file:///C:/DocumentNoSnc/CC/GXEMUL/GXEMUL-SEC/src/cpus/memory_rw.c#L784), returning `MEMORY_ACCESS_OK`.
