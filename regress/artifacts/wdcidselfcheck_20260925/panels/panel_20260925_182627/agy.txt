#### Question 2: The Adjudication's Caution on In-File Trailer Pin
> *Can an in-file pin on `main()`'s trailer survive ONE inlining edit and ONE inserted conditional reset (`if (failures == cmp_fails) failures = cmp_fails = 0;`)?*

**Answer:** **No, it cannot.**
1. Any in-file pin on `main()`'s trailer relies on wrapping the trailer into a probed function (e.g. `report_verdict()`).
2. Because `main()` is the entry point, a single edit can inline or replace `report_verdict(...)` with direct printfs or bypass it entirely. Probes in `selfcheck()` cannot detect whether `main()` actually executed the helper or inlined it away.
3. The second edit inserts `if (failures == cmp_fails) failures = cmp_fails = 0;` before the verdict printf. Under covered-only defects (`drqring`, `w57hi`), `failures == cmp_fails` holds, wiping both tallies to 0. Under the self-mutant (`selfmut`), the geometry row is inline and increments `failures` alone, so `failures == 1` and `cmp_fails == 0`, leaving the reset inactive and keeping the self-mutant lane green.
4. Hence, the trailer class cannot be closed in-file. The adjudication's single-call-site reasoning is mathematically sound, and Class (3) correctly treats this as a disclosed contract limitation whose true closure is gate-side (`wdcidlane`).

#### Question 3: The Call-Count Pin
> *Does it catch every way of running a covered row after the verdict? Is there a way to move covered rows past the verdict that keeps `chk()` at 24 and `row_cmp()` at 8 by the last self-check? Does it change any healthy run's output beyond SELFCHECK lines?*

**Answer:**
1. **Yes, it catches every way of running a covered row after the verdict.** Any move of a covered row past the verdict removes its call before [`selfcheck("last")`](file:///C:/Users/User00/AppData/Local/Temp/claude/C--DocumentNoSnc-cc-GXEMUL/8e474f41-4d47-4c99-bf11-3406a84c4fcd/scratchpad/wdc551/MINE_e.c#L5832), leaving `chk_calls < 24` or `rowcmp_calls < 8`.
2. **There is no single move that keeps the counts at 24 and 8.** Restoring the counts would require inserting artificial probe/dummy calls earlier in `main()`, which requires multiple coordinated edits (a composite). Moving [`selfcheck("last")`](file:///C:/Users/User00/AppData/Local/Temp/claude/C--DocumentNoSnc-cc-GXEMUL/8e474f41-4d47-4c99-bf11-3406a84c4fcd/scratchpad/wdc551/MINE_e.c#L5832) along with the rows leaves `sc_g == 0` at the verdict, forcing `WDC_IDENTIFY_FAIL`.
3. **No healthy run's output is changed beyond SELFCHECK lines.** Verified in [`run551e.out:3-16`](file:///C:/DocumentNoSnc/cc/GXEMUL/GXEMUL-SEC/regress/artifacts/wdcidselfcheck_20260925/measured/run551e.out#L3-L16): stripping lines matching `SELFCHECK` produces byte-identical output to `ORIG` across all 7 device variants under both `-O2` and `-O0`.
