**Zero floor breaches found outside the disclosed classes.**

**Denominator (attack classes evaluated & proven closed):**
1. **Single-tally resets (`failures = 0;` or `cmp_fails = 0;`):**

In `#551d`, the verdict and exit code bypass `failures` reconciliation and read `cmp_fails` directly (measured: [`run551d_r2.out:50-53`](file:///C:/DocumentNoSnc/cc/GXEMUL/GXEMUL-SEC/regress/artifacts/wdcidselfcheck_20260925/measured/run551d_r2.out#L50-L53) shows `MINE_recon2` and `MINE_subafter` are rc=1, 0 PASS on `drqring` and `w57hi`).

   * Narrowing to 16/32-bit (`uint16_t`) trips wide probes 4 & 5 (`0x100000000ULL`) (measured: `MINE_u16` rc=1).
   * Inverting `!=` to `<` (`lt`) or adding `&& sectors` (`andsect`) trips probes 6 & 7 (`sectors = 0`) (measured: `MINE_lt` and `MINE_andsect` rc=1).

#### Q6. What Did All of Us Miss?
1. **Compensating Probe Hole in `row_cmp()`:**
   All 8 `row_cmp()` probes feature either a single wrong disjunct or both disjuncts wrong in the *same* direction (Probe 2: both below). No probe presents disjuncts wrong in *opposite* directions. Adding probe 8 `(sectors 2, got57 3, got60 1)` requires 1 line in `rw`, `r57`, `r60` and permanently closes all compensating sum relations.
2. **Fragile Prefix Check in `selfcheck()`:**
   `if (where[0] == 'f')` at line 259 matches `"first"`, but would also match `"fail"`, `"final"`, or `"freeze"`. Using `strcmp(where, "first") == 0` is strictly safer.
3. **Reconciliation Line Stdout Disconnect:**
   With F1's direct check of `cmp_fails`, the reconciliation line `if (cmp_fails > failures) failures = cmp_fails;` is non-load-bearing for exit status. When an edit like `recon2` or `subafter` zeroes `failures`, the binary prints `128 rows, 0 failures` immediately followed by `WDC_IDENTIFY_FAIL`. While exit code 1 prevents a false pass, printing `0 failures` alongside a failed verdict is confusing. Replacing the print tally with `total_fails = (cmp_fails > failures) ? cmp_fails : failures;` makes stdout self-consistent.
