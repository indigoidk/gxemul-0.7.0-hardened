## 1. Clause (d)

### rlwimi — edited lines

`uint32_t tmp = (uint32_t)reg(...)` and `if (mb > me) ra = (ra & 0xffffffffULL) | ((uint64_t)tmp << 32)`.

Dominating predicates up to the function boundary: `mb > me` (the added guard); `sh ?` (produces `tmp`); the walk loop’s `mb == me` / `mb == 32` do **not** dominate the high-word line (it runs first — that is why `R_POST_LOOP` is a null of the repair). Body of the `if` reads `ra` and `tmp`.

Floor cells that are actually owed: `{wrap, nowrap}` × `{sh=0, sh≠0}`. All four are rowed (`wrap mutant` / `pres wrap` / `wrap fullword`; `wrap sh8 mb20` / `wrap sh16 mb28` / `alias both`; `nowrap sh0`; `nowrap sh8 mb0` and siblings). The loop’s `mb == me` re-test makes `{mb==me}` enumeratable; that cell is in the census, not in any named pattern row.

## 2. Channel vacuity

No target row is vacuous under the mutant it is named for.

- **rldimi census / s1 / s2, source `~0`:** blind to **every** rotate-amount and rotate-direction defect (`ROTL64(~0, k) = ~0`). Preserve is also blind (dest 0). They still move under mask-bound mutants (`D_MUT63`, `D_NOWRAP`, `D_NOCIRC`). Rotate is carried only by `pres nowrap` (SH=8) and `pres wrap` (SH=40); `pres sh0` pins “no rotate at SH=0,” not a nonzero rotate.
- **`nowrap sh0` / `nowrap sh0 mb0`:** agree with HEAD by construction (SH=0 skips the rlwimi rotate; rldimi SH=0 mask is 0..63 either way). They are target-instruction **controls**, and they do kill `R_NULL` / `D_NULL` / `R_REV_COND`.
- **`wrap mutant` (SH=0):** cannot see width contamination. It is named for the high half; `R_ONLY_CAST` moves it.
- **`cr0 record form`:** presence of `cr=` only, both probes. Disclosed on the rldimi side.
- **`bits32 rlwimi`:** low 32 only; high-half repair is invisible on PPC603. Reachability, not a product kill.

## 4. Book I (the fixes, not the detectors)

Neither handler is wrong against Book I v2.01 for an unreached `(SH,MB,ME)`.

- **MASK** at :578 wraps iff `x > y`. **ROTL32** at :501/:4008 is `x\|\|x` then rotate. **rlwimi** RTL :4316–4319 is `RA <- r&m | (RA)&~m` with `m = MASK(MB+32, ME+32)`. For every `MB>ME`, `ME+32 ≥ 32`, so bits 0:31 are entirely in the mask — replacing the whole high word with the rotated low word is exact, not an approximation. `MB==ME` is a single low-word bit; `>` is the right test (`>=` is not). The 32-bit walk was already the low-word insert; it was not this round’s defect.
- **rldimi** RTL `MASK(b, ~n)` is `MASK(mb, 63-SH)`. The loop is do-while over `[mb, successor(63-SH))` mod 64. `(64-sh)&63` is that successor, including `SH=0 → 0` (full word from `mb` through 63). Saving `sh` in `n` is required; `while (sh-- != 0)` really does leave `sh == -1` for every SH.
- Census is 129 / 32768 and 495 / 4096. Both algorithms are uniform in the instruction fields; there is no per-cell branch that could be right on the sampled set and wrong off it. 64-bit CPU in 32-bit mode remains the filed hole (`ppccr0modewidth`).

S1.2 :342 (“instructions that set a 64-bit register affect all 64 bits … independent of mode”) is what the new rlwimi comment cites; I checked it. That comment is not claiming MODE32 register width is architectural, and it says so.
