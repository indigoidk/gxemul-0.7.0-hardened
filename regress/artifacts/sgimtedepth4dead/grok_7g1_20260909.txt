## Q1

**Yes, the switch dominates. No, that does not make A/B/C three floor cells.** Premise 6 excludes the per-label split. “Dispatch axes are full axes” is not the sentence this answer relies on.

Quote, clause (1):

> DEPENDS ON enumerates exactly two things: (1) the predicates DOMINATING the diff's edited lines, walked up only to the ENCLOSING FUNCTION BOUNDARY -- never into callees or callers

Every path to `if (depth == 32)` goes through `switch (dst_tlb)`. The switch dominates.

`case MTE_TLB_A` does **not**. Dominance means every path: a path through B reaches the edit without A. The three labels fall through into one block *before* the edited line. The dominating predicate is membership in the tiled group, `dst_tlb ∈ {A,B,C}` — not three predicates.

Quote, pass-10 (this is the sentence that actually decides the split):

> DEPENDS-ON enumerates JOINT cells over the guard's operands and the body-read lvalues EXACTLY WHEN THE GUARDED BODY'S OWN CODE DISTINGUISHES THEM.

The guarded body is the two `\|=`. It does not test `dst_tlb`. Fine values A vs B vs C inside the joined group are exactly the class pass-10 already sent to (e):

> A value or combination the body never tests as a case (`idata == k`; a byte no conditional distinguishes; …) is DISCLOSED under clause (e).

Quote, pass-11 (floor, not enumeration):

> The FLOOR -- the cells a row is OWED for -- is the cross-product of the guard's dominating predicates with the device-STATE lvalues the fix's own repair path reads that are JOINTLY REACHABLE through its own data flow. … A filter may reject or discharge a cell but may demand a new row only for a floor cell.

The repair path reads `src_mode` and `dst_mode` (locals) and a constant. It reads no device-state `dst_tlb`. The depth axis is the guard’s own operand and is already rowed (`depth8` / `depth16` / fill-at-32 / `depth64`). LINEAR and `default` are other arms of the same switch; LINEAR `exit(1)`s at depth 32; they are not this floor.

“Dispatch axes as full axes” is the measuring seat’s bound, left as a named residual. Treating it as adopted law would make pass-10’s “EXACTLY WHEN” surplusage.

## Q4 (grok half)

**M4 and B/C are not the same sentence once the two clauses are split.**

**M4** `if (depth == 32 && (mode & MTE_MODE_COPY))`:
- Clause (1): `mode & MTE_MODE_COPY` is tested *later*, inside the pixel loop. It dominates nothing about the edited line. **Fail.**
- Clause (2): the guarded body is the two `\|=`; it never reads COPY. **Fail.**
- Both fail → (e). Filing it as (d) would import the dual `&& !COPY` into the floor, and that dual cannot be killed honestly by this instrument (pass-2, byte-walked). That half stands.

**B/C** `if (depth == 32 && dst_tlb == MTE_TLB_A)`:
- Clause (1): `switch (dst_tlb)` **does** dominate. Individual labels **do not** (join, above). The dominating predicate is the tiled *group*. Adding `== MTE_TLB_A` refines a cell the body never splits; it is not “Y drawn from a dominating predicate” in the `#488` sense, because `== A` was never a gate on the line.
- Clause (2): the two `\|=` do not read `dst_tlb`. Operand-swap of the labels **fails**. (`dst_tlb` *is* read when *constructing* `dst_mode`, outside the guarded body and used for array selection in a callee clause (1) forbids entering.)
- Clause (1) applies to the group; it does not enumerate the per-label split. Pass-10’s “EXACTLY WHEN” is what enumerates that split, and it does not fire.

Pass-1’s one-liner treated B/C as M4. That wording is wrong. The disposition is not: B/C remain (e), `fill b top` remains cheap-not-required (and, as shipped, *absent*). The complete reason is the join plus “the body does not re-test the selector,” not “the type OR does not read `dst_tlb`” alone.

The 72-combination count is not mine. It counts the *input space* the tiled arm admits (9 src/dst pairs × fill/copy × 4 depths). Pass-11 rejected that bound. The shipped rows drive **AA fill at 8/16/32/64 and AA copy at 32** — five of those 72, which is a contract fact already in the CHANGELOG’s codex sentence, not a floor debt.
