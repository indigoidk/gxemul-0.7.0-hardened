defect chain that nobody had connected, so the function deserves reading as a whole rather than at
the one site.

**The established chain, verified — treat it as context, and correct it if it is wrong:**

    src_mode = (src_tlb << 10) + (((mode & MTE_MODE_DEPTH_MASK) >> MTE_DEPTH_SHIFT) << 8);
    dst_mode = (dst_tlb << 10) + (((mode & MTE_MODE_DEPTH_MASK) >> MTE_DEPTH_SHIFT) << 8);

The constructed modes carry no colour bits, so the colour type is `0x00` (CI). The only thing that
would make them RGBA is that condition, which cannot be true. So the transfer always runs CI — and
CI's pixel-write arm assigns only `buf[0]` while the transfer length is `bufdepth` bytes, which is
2 or 4 at the documented `MTE_DEPTH_16` / `MTE_DEPTH_32`.

For **Q2**, the additional findings are:

1. **Both Y-step registers are ignored by traversal.** `src_y_step` is only logged. `dst_y_step` is logged and compared against `0`, `1`, and `-1`, but never controls an address or increment. Destination endpoint ordering determines `dy`, and source Y advances by that same `dy`. Other destination steps produce a warning and continue. Zero and negative programmed steps therefore do not create a zero-increment loop; their requested behavior is simply absent. See [step handling](C:/DocumentNoSnc/CC/GXEMUL/GXEMUL-SEC/src/devices/dev_sgi_re.c:1300) and [traversal](C:/DocumentNoSnc/CC/GXEMUL/GXEMUL-SEC/src/devices/dev_sgi_re.c:1353).

2. **`SRC1` has no operational effect.** It is loaded and logged; its coordinate extraction is commented out.
