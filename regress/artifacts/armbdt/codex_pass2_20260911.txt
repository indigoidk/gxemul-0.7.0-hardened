   Consider a four-byte access starting at `B - 1`, where `B` is a page boundary:

   | Version | Bytes requested | Translation behavior |
   |---|---|---|
   | Old | `B - 1` through `B + 2` | Can access both mappings |
   | New | `B - 4` through `B - 1` | Accesses only the preceding mapping |
