## 2. Form and placement of `addr & 0xfffffffc`

Form is right: for `uint32_t`, `0xfffffffc` ≡ `~3` with no C99 promotion hazard on any plausible host [REASONED]. Placement: **virtual, before translation, per-register — and masking after translation would be strictly worse, twice over.** (a) Alignment is a property of the *architectural* (virtual) address; the fast path masks virtual, so post-translation masking re-opens fast/slow divergence in physical space for any translation whose frame base isn't 4-aligned [REASONED]. (b) Translating the raw address first walks — and faults on — pages the architectural access never touches; the spurious faults finding 6 describes would then persist. Finding 6 is therefore a description of the fix being *right*: the fault that disappears is one the architecture says cannot occur.
