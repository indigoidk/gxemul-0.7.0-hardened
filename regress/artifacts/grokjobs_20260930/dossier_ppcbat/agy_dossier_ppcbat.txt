### (a) Chosen Disposition Member and CPU Machinery Evaluation

The dossier chooses **Member 5 (Architectural fault)**, combined with **Member 7 (Implement missing behavior)** to complete required exception delivery state.

#### 1. Is Member 5 Right?
Yes. Motorola PEM32 §7.5.4 ([`_scratchpad/ppc_pem32_rev2_2001.txt:13312, 13334–13336`](file:///C:/DocumentNoSnc/CC/GXEMUL/_scratchpad/ppc_pem32_rev2_2001.txt#L13312)) mandates that when an effective address matches a valid BAT block entry and the PP bits prohibit the access (`PP=00`, or a store attempted under `PP=01` or `PP=11`), the processor must generate a block protection violation exception condition:
- A Data Storage Interrupt (DSI) for data accesses, with bit 4 of DSISR set, DAR loaded with the faulting address, and SRR0 loaded with the causing instruction's address.
- An Instruction Storage Interrupt (ISI) for instruction accesses, with bit 4 of SRR1 set and SRR0 loaded with the attempted fetch address.
