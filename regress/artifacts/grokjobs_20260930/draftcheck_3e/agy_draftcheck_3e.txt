**(a) Findings that are wrong or overstated**

3. `devm8820x:F3` misattributed the defect mechanism: the reviewer framed the missing dirty-bit updates as an effect of removing incidental CMMU register purges. In reality, the root cause is upstream in `src/cpus/memory_m88k.c:370`, which returns 2 (writable) on read translations, causing `src/cpus/memory_rw.c:746` to populate `host_store[]` on reads; subsequent stores bypass the MMU translation path entirely.
