### (a) Dossier's Chosen Disposition Member and Delivery Machinery

The dossier selects **Member 7** (*implement missing behavior: bounded exception dispatch and safe return to scheduling*) while maintaining **Member 5** (*fault the guest*) as the architectural memory access disposition.

1. **Correctness of the disposition:**
   This choice is correct. Member 5 is the architecturally mandated response for unmapped or unauthorized memory accesses (ARM DDI 0100I A2.6.5). Merely dropping the abort (Member 2), latching and continuing (Member 3), or inventing mappings (Member 6) contradicts architectural specifications. However, Member 5 alone is already what `memory_arm.c:268-276` attempts; the defect is that exception dispatch invokes `quick_pc_to_pointers(cpu)` synchronously from within the faulting call stack, causing recursive delivery. Therefore, Member 7 (implementing bounded exception dispatch that returns to the outer execution loop without call stack recursion) is required to deliver Member 5 safely.

2. **Presence of exception delivery machinery on this CPU:**
   The architectural state delivery machinery for Prefetch Abort is **fully present** in committed source at [`src/cpus/cpu_arm.c:616-734`](file:///C:/DocumentNoSnc/cc/GXEMUL/GXEMUL-SEC/src/cpus/cpu_arm.c#L616-L734):
   - **Range check:** Validates `exception_nr` against `N_ARM_EXCEPTIONS` at [`src/cpus/cpu_arm.c:620-624`](file:///C:/DocumentNoSnc/cc/GXEMUL/GXEMUL-SEC/src/cpus/cpu_arm.c#L620-L624).
