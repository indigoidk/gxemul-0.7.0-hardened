### (a) Chosen Disposition Member and Supporting Machinery

**Verdict on Member Choice:**
The dossier's chosen disposition, **Member 3 (decline unsupported B-bit changes while preserving configured byte order, announcing once per CPU instance)**, is the correct, bounded, and architecturally conformant resolution.
