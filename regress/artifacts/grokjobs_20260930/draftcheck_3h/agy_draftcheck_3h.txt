   In [src/promemul/arcbios.c:1010-1011](file:///C:/DocumentNoSnc/cc/GXEMUL/GXEMUL-SEC/src/promemul/arcbios.c#L1010-L1011), `st = (buf[8] + (buf[9] << 8) + (buf[10] << 16) + (buf[11] << 24)) * 512;` evaluates with signed 32-bit `int` promotion. When `buf[11] >= 0x80` (values 128 to 255), `buf[11] << 24` triggers signed integer overflow UB. For non-negative assembled values exceeding `2,147,483,647 / 512 = 4,194,303` (i.e. sector offsets $\ge 4,194,304$, corresponding to a 2 GiB partition offset), multiplication by 512 also overflows signed `int`. However, at [src/promemul/arcbios.c:1024](file:///C:/DocumentNoSnc/cc/GXEMUL/GXEMUL-SEC/src/promemul/arcbios.c#L1024), the guard checks `if (st == 0 || offset + st <= offset || ++ebr_chain_links > 128)`. On platforms where signed overflow wraps negative, `offset + st <= offset` evaluates to `true` and actively rejects the link. Thus, signed overflow does not universally defeat the EBR loop bound, though arithmetic UB remains present.
