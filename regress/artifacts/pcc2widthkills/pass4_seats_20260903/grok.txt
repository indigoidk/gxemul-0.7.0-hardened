**Odd-block RDDECL UNREACHABLE — true.** Guest: `#ifndef LS_1` alignment-faults before `memory_rw`; `relative_addr %= 0x40` keeps parity. Debugger reads (`dump` `:716`, fill `:1006`, unasm `:1918`) all pass `NO_EXCEPTIONS`. `put b` is `memory_rw(..., 1, MEM_WRITE, CACHE_NONE | NO_EXCEPTIONS)`. ## 4. Is the record honest?
