PASS 3b raises one disposition question, and both sides deserve stating.

THE REPAIR'S OWN PRETABLE (SECTION 2) DISCLOSES:

  "Asymmetry left, DISCLOSED: PSCALEADJ/SCCICR/SCCRX/SCSIICR have no readback row.
   Their `dcl' rows red under any guard edit that lets the halfword land silently
   (len > 2, delete, operand swap) -- every shape in this table.  A guard edit that
   lands the write AND still announces (the M_FALL_MASK shape) at one of those four
   would escape; that is the sentence owed, and MASK's own fall-through is the
   measured instance of the shape."

That is an honest, specific, predicted escape -- exactly what clause (e) is for.

READING A (the round's): clause (e) says a contract declares what the detector
catches, so a DISCLOSED escape is "outside the contract" rather than a breach of it.
Disposition: coverage debt, FILE it, rank below live defects.

READING B (mine): clause (d) is a FLOOR clause.  The floor is the MINIMUM a detector
must clear -- "the detector must also fail when the fix is PRESENT but its REPAIR IS
NULLED -- the guard kept, its effect removed."  M_FALL at one of those four arms is
exactly that: guard kept, announce kept, decline nulled, write lands.  Clause (e)
governs claims ABOVE the floor; you cannot disclose your way BELOW it, or every floor
breach becomes dischargeable by writing a sentence.  Disposition: L2, in-round.

WHY IT IS WORTH MEASURING RATHER THAN ARGUING: the shape's blast radius differs by arm.
At SCCICR/SCCRX/SCSIICR the fall-through body is `= idata & 0x1f' (or 0xdf) FOLLOWED BY
reassert_interrupts() -- so a landed wide write puts the neighbour's lane into an
INTERRUPT CONTROL register and reasserts.  At PSCALEADJ the body only prints.  So the
four are not equivalent, and "one sentence covers all four" may itself be too coarse.

MEASURED HERE: M_FALL_SCCICR, M_FALL_SCCRX, M_FALL_SCSIICR, M_FALL_PSCALEADJ against
the frozen 78-row detector.  Result decides nothing about the reading -- it establishes
whether the escape is real, which is the part that is checkable.

--- UPDATED after M6 was MEASURED (77/78, RED `rb mask' ALONE) ---

The M6 measurement changes the character of the question.  Before it, "a fall-through at
one of those four would escape" was a PREDICTION in a pre-table block.  Now the mechanism
is measured: under announce-then-land the announce survives, so `latch all' reads 12 and
the arm's `dcl' row reads 1, and the readback is the sole surviving channel.

That makes the four-arm gap STRUCTURAL rather than incidental.  It is not "a mutant we
happened not to build"; it is "a shape for which four of the twelve arms have no channel
of any kind".

It also sharpens what the fix costs to close, which matters for the disposition: a
readback at SCCICR/SCCRX/SCSIICR is ONE session each of the shape already used for
`rb scctx' (st.b a known byte at the legal width; put h a wide value; ld.bu it back) --
except that these three are ODD offsets, so the wide write must go through the debugger
and the readback through a guest ld.bu, exactly as `rb mask' already does.  So the
pattern exists in the file and costs three copies of it.  PSCALEADJ stores nothing, so it
genuinely needs no readback and its `dcl' row is the honest channel -- that arm belongs
in the DOES-NOT-CATCH sentence, not in a new row.

So the practical answer may be neither "L2 now" nor "file it": THREE rows and ONE
sentence, which is smaller than the argument about which it is.
