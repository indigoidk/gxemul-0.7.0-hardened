**2. Five are storage-only; CONF has exactly one additional consumer.**

Searching the field names and `struct lca_data` throughout the allowed tree found no additional uses. The five non-CONF fields have exactly the readbacks listed above.

`ioc_conf` has **two reads total**: its register readback and `bus = d->ioc_conf & 1` in [`DEVICE_ACCESS(lca_pci_conf)`](C:/DocumentNoSnc/CC/GXEMUL/GXEMUL-SEC/src/devices/dev_lca.c:121). Extra bits are discarded **for bus selection**, while the IOC readback returns the stored value. Masking the store would therefore change the proposal’s readback policy; the existing bus consumer does not require it.

The ordering establishes what has been assigned, but does not establish a successful-write contract: currently the rejected paths terminate. Your change deliberately lets them reach `return 1` and makes their stored values available to subsequent reads. [`DEVICE_ACCESS(lca_ioc)`](C:/DocumentNoSnc/CC/GXEMUL/GXEMUL-SEC/src/devices/dev_lca.c:327)
