**Q4 — Worse direction.** **Silent acceptance is more dangerous** (REASONED). Termination is loud and attributable: a developer sees `fatal` + `exit(1)` and knows the emulator is at fault; the guest's intent is unambiguous from the write. Silent acceptance is unobservable — the emulator stores the value (or doesn't), and the guest's PCI windows either go nowhere or map to whatever hardcoded region the decode uses, a Heisenbug that can silently misroute DMA. Your hint that "the model's window matching may not use these registers at all" makes this sharper: if decode ignores IOC_W_MASK, silent acceptance guarantees the guest's intended mapping is silently dropped, with no signal that anything went wrong. Termination at least fails closed.
