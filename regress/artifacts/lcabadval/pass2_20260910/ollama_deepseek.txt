**Q3. OPTION-SET COMPLETENESS**

Yes, a member is missing.  
For **W_MASK**: the design declines invalid encodings and keeps the previous value. An unnamed option is to **store the raw or masked value without field validation** and announce once that window translation is not modelled. Since translation is not modelled anyway, the field rule is not behaviourally necessary; declining is a policy choice, not required by the manual.  
For **default arm**: the design declines writes and answers reads with zero. An unnamed option is to **store writes to a per-offset dummy register and return reads from that dummy**, announcing once per offset. The precedents named on pass 1 (`dev_gt.c` stored-but-unused address-decode registers, `dev_footbridge.c` SDRAM_BA_MASK) suggest this stored-but-unused idiom.
