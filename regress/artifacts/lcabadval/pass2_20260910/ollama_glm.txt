## Q1 — premise audit

**(a) READ:** the field is `f = (idata & 0xfff00000) >> 20`, so the domain is the 4096 values 0x000–0xfff; `f+1` cannot overflow (max 0x1000 in 64-bit). **REASONED, enumerated:** for any f, let t be its lowest zero bit; f+1 clears bits 0..t−1 and sets bit t, so `f & (f+1)` equals f's bits *above* t. That is zero iff f has no set bit above its lowest zero — i.e. f = 2^k − 1. Walking k = 0..12: **0x000, 0x001, 0x003, 0x007, 0x00f, 0x01f, 0x03f, 0x07f, 0x0ff, 0x1ff, 0x3ff, 0x7ff, 0xfff** — thirteen values; every other f fails (spot-walk: f=5, 5&6=4≠0; f=6, 6&7=6≠0; f=7, 7&8=0 ✓).
