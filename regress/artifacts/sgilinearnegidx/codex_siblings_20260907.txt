I also found these separate issues:

- **TEX indexing overlaps writes.** Successive aligned writes address `0–3`, `1–4`, through `27–30`. Elements **31–111** are never addressed by this handler. This establishes an indexing/mapping anomaly without assuming the intended hardware mapping.
- **Pixel-buffer initialization does not match transfer width.** CI PUT initializes one byte; other PUT branches initialize four, while the transfer may consume eight. Non-CI GET expressions use bytes through index 3 even when only one or two bytes were read. The allocation is large enough; initialization is incomplete. See [line 284](C:/DocumentNoSnc/CC/GXEMUL/GXEMUL-SEC/src/devices/dev_sgi_re.c:284).
- **Stipple rotation can have a negative shift count.** Walking all 1,024 start/max-field pairs gives rotation counts **−31 through 31**, with **496 negative cases**. These are undefined C shifts, independent of array bounds. See [calculation](C:/DocumentNoSnc/CC/GXEMUL/GXEMUL-SEC/src/devices/dev_sgi_re.c:687) and [use](C:/DocumentNoSnc/CC/GXEMUL/GXEMUL-SEC/src/devices/dev_sgi_re.c:843).
- **Signed arithmetic remains unsafe elsewhere.** `src_x += step_x` can overflow; the earlier step diagnostic does not reject execution. `saved_src_x` also truncates the source coordinate to `uint16_t` before each rectangle row. See [line 757](C:/DocumentNoSnc/CC/GXEMUL/GXEMUL-SEC/src/devices/dev_sgi_re.c:757) and [line 912](C:/DocumentNoSnc/CC/GXEMUL/GXEMUL-SEC/src/devices/dev_sgi_re.c:912).
- **Value-building left shifts have signed-promotion problems.** On 32-bit-int C, the TLB `uint16_t << 16` expressions and pixel `uint8_t << 24` expressions can produce results unrepresentable as signed `int`. These occur after safe subscript selection.
- **MTE has two plainly inconsistent conditions:** `else if (len != 4)` accepts every remaining width, making its final rejection arm unreachable; `depth == 4` is also unreachable because the enumerated depths are **8, 16, 32, 64**. See [width handling](C:/DocumentNoSnc/CC/GXEMUL/GXEMUL-SEC/src/devices/dev_sgi_re.c:1499) and [depth comparison](C:/DocumentNoSnc/CC/GXEMUL/GXEMUL-SEC/src/devices/dev_sgi_re.c:1348).
