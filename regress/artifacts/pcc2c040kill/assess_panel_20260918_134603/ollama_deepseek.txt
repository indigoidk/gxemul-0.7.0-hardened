**Q2 — Severity and fix choice**

Host termination on a legal guest byte store is a high-severity denial-of-service. The correct immediate fix is to remove the fatal exit and suppress the unsupported delivery, with a latched announcement. That is exactly what the shipped fix does. Full EIPL implementation would be a better long-term fix but is not required to close the defect.
