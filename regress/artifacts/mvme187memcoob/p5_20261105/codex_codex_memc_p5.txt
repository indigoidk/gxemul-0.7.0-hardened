The default path tests warned_default and writeflag, and prints offset, controller and idata. It reads neither warned_memconf nor any served register field.

The controller-fold branch rejoins before the switch. Its true branch does not dominate the notice bodies. The two latches are individually reachable and can coexist, but that does not make their cross-product a state partition read by either repair path.

2. NB4

For chiprev and memconf, NB4 really causes no field-value change on the shipped probe. It is not an unobserved corruption.

I checked the actual declarations: both are volatile u_char fields. The controller-0 first memconf stores enumerate as:

wr cnf: 0x07
R3: 0xff
R5: 0x07
R8: 0x07
R12: 0xffffffff
R13: 0x07
R15: 0x07
R16: 0x07
R17: 0x07

The relevant byte calculations are:

0x01 & 0x07 = 0x01
0x01 & 0xff = 0x01
0x03 & 0x07 = 0x03
0x03 & 0xff = 0x03

R7 and R9 first store through controller 1, so !controller rejects them. Later controller-0 zero stores bypass the already-set memconf latch body.

Chipid is different: 0x80 & 0x07 = 0. Its NB4 variant is detected by R16 100b r12, R16 00b r13, R16 100c r18 and R16 00c r19.

This is probe-relative NOCHANGE, not semantic equivalence for all inputs. A first controller-0 zero store would change chiprev and memconf. Such an experiment could discharge the debt, but it is not owed by this floor adjudication.
