## Premises I cannot settle from the packet

Whether a physical address with no registered device causes `memory_rw` to raise a fault (which would make the row green) or to return a non-fault failure code that the handler then swallows (which would leave the row red) is **not determined by anything inlined in §2 or §3**. 2. **`CACHE_NONE | PHYSICAL` ≡ bare `PHYSICAL` at this call site.** The brief explicitly admits it has not enumerated the two RAM-path conditions in `memory_rw.c` that read `cache`. (READ -- the brief flags this itself.)
3. **"Every existing LCA probe runs with ptbr == 0."** Stated, not enumerated. Plausible (the function only works at all under that condition), but the brief asks the panel to take it on trust.
