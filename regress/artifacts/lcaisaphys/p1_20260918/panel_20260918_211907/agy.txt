## 3. Adversarial Review of Section 6 Questions

### Q1. Premise Audit
1. **Could the nested address legitimately be virtual?**
   **No.** The nested address is synthesized entirely within the emulator by adding `LCA_ISA_BASE = 0x1d0000000` to the decoded 16-bit ISA port ([`dev_lca.c:47, 301, 305`](file:///C:/DocumentNoSnc/CC/GXEMUL/GXEMUL-SEC/src/devices/dev_lca.c#L47)). ISA devices (`ns16550`, `i8042`, `mc146818`, etc.) are registered in physical memory at `0x1d0000000` by [`bus_isa_init`](file:///C:/DocumentNoSnc/CC/GXEMUL/GXEMUL-SEC/src/devices/dev_lca.c#L568-L570). A guest never requests `0x1d0000000`; it requests a sparse cycle at `0x1c0000000 + (port << 5)`. Handing `0x1d0000000` to virtual translation looks up an unmapped 33-bit user VA in the guest's page table.
2. **Does any other device forward with a missing `PHYSICAL`?**
   **No.** Across all of `src/devices/*.c`, only 3 `memory_rw` calls omit `PHYSICAL`: the two in [`dev_lca.c:301, 305`](file:///C:/DocumentNoSnc/CC/GXEMUL/GXEMUL-SEC/src/devices/dev_lca.c#L301), and one in `dev_ether.c:188` (which handles guest-provided buffer DMA pointers, not internal device-to-device MMIO forwards). `dev_lca.c` is the sole device performing nested MMIO bridge forwarding without `PHYSICAL`.
3. **Severity Assessment (MED vs HIGH):**
   **HIGH.** While localized to the LCA chipset (AlphaBook1, Noname/AXPpci33), the failure renders the entire ISA bus completely dead the moment any guest OS boots and arms paging via `CALL_PAL swpctx`. Console serial, keyboard, mouse, and RTC fail, while unlatched `fatal()` messages flood host stderr on every guest port poll. For the affected machines, it is fatal to OS execution.

### Q2. Candidate Weakenings Evaluation
- **(a) `PHYSICAL` on WRITE call only:**
  - *Equivalence:* **DIFFERENT.**
  - *First divergence:* First sparse read after paging arm (row 9, `UART_LSR` at port `0x3fd`).
  - *Outcome:* Row 9 (`sp lsr`) FAILS (returns false 0x00); Row 11 (`r miss`) FAILS (translator prints miss). Rows 10 and 12 are green. Score: 11/13.
- **(b) `PHYSICAL` on READ call only:**
  - *Equivalence:* **DIFFERENT.**
  - *First divergence:* First sparse write after paging arm (row 10, `UART_SCR` at port `0x3ff`).
  - *Outcome:* Row 10 (`w lands`) FAILS (scratch register not updated); Row 12 (`w miss`) FAILS (miss message printed). Rows 9 and 11 are green. Score: 11/13.
- **(c) `NO_EXCEPTIONS | PHYSICAL` on both:**
  - *Equivalence:* **DIFFERENT (Catastrophic Regression).**
  - *First divergence:* Row 2 (`live`, 8042 self-test write to `SP(KBC_CMD)` before arming).
  - *Outcome:* In [`memory_rw.c:584`](file:///C:/DocumentNoSnc/CC/GXEMUL/GXEMUL-SEC/src/cpus/memory_rw.c#L584), device access is guarded by `if (!no_exceptions || (mem->devices[i].flags & DM_READS_HAVE_NO_SIDE_EFFECTS))`. ISA devices are registered with `DM_DEFAULT` (0). Passing `NO_EXCEPTIONS` causes device handlers to be skipped entirely. Row 2 fails to get `0x55`, tripping the `CONTROL=DEAD` guard. Score: 0/0 (`LCAISAPHYS_CONTROL=DEAD`).
- **(d) Bare `PHYSICAL` (dropping `CACHE_NONE`) on both:**
  - *Equivalence:* **EQUIVALENT.**
  - *First divergence:* None.
  - *Outcome:* As proven in §2 above, `cache` is never inspected on any path reachable by an ISA device address. Leaves all 13 rows green (13/13). The proposal retains `CACHE_NONE` purely as the minimal 1-token-per-site delta.
- **(e) `PHYSICAL` only when `relative_addr < 0x200`:**
  - *Equivalence:* **DIFFERENT.**
  - *First divergence:* Row 9 (port `0x3fd >= 0x200`).
  - *Outcome:* Standard ISA peripherals live at ports $\ge 0x200$ (COM1 at $0x3f8$, COM2 at $0x2f8$, LPT at $0x378$, floppy at $0x3f0$). Leaves defect rows 9–12 RED. Score: 9/13.

### Q3. Option-Set Completeness
- Option (1) (`CACHE_NONE | PHYSICAL` on the two calls) is the smallest, least invasive, and strictly correct fix.
- Option (2) (Direct device callback) requires bypassing the memory bus infrastructure and reimplementing device lookup inside `dev_lca.c`.
- Option (3) (Registering ISA devices directly at sparse strides) breaks the architecture of `bus_isa.c`, which assumes standard contiguous ISA port mapping.
- Option (4) (Synthesizing K0SEG addresses) leaks CPU segmentation concepts into PCI/ISA bridge code and needlessly invokes translation.
Option (1) is the only clean and minimal option.

### Q4. Witness Audit
1. **Control Claims:**
   - `alive`: verifies host survives without abort.
   - `live`: verifies end-to-end sparse window forwarding and device response while `ptbr == 0`.
   - `flat lsr`: verifies UART hardware is alive at physical `0x1d00003fd` via flat mapping.
   - `ram plant`: verifies physical RAM backing under identity mapping.
   - `ptbr miss`: verifies that after `swpctx`, raw virtual addresses walk the page table and miss.
   - `ptbr sent`: verifies walking load fails to complete into destination register.
   - `pte2 todo`: verifies the walk aborted at level 1 without hitting unhandled PTE2 logic.
2. **`CONTROL=DEAD` Guard:**
   Sound and necessary. A broken `swpctx` or failed paging setup would leave identity mapping active, causing the defect rows to falsely pass on an unpatched binary. The guard prevents false green reporting.
