| Location | 8W | 8R | 4W | 4R |

| REV 0x00, pc *outside* the window | sd | ld | sw | lw |
| REV 0x00, pc *in* `0xbfc051xx` | **sdP** | **missing (`ldP`)** | **swP** | lwP |

| default: 4-byte high 0x40, 4-byte low 0x34 and 0x04, 8-byte 0x40 and 0x278 | yes | yes | yes | yes |

The width arm accepts only `len==8` and `len==4`. Case labels are 8-aligned, so a 4-byte access at a label is the high lane (`relative_addr & 4 == 0`); a 4-byte access at `label+4` does **not** take that case, it takes `default`. Those 4-byte-low-at-label cells are unreachable. Correctly omitted.

CONTROL's `HARD_RESET` true side is disclosed (it stops the CPUs). `is_32bit` is a machine constant on this probe (Opus close-4 named it and did not build it).

The hole is one cell of the same product pass 4 demanded: **kind × location**. The REV window is an `if` both directions and both widths can take (`cpu->pc` only). Repair 10 put three window kinds in the list — `lwP` (4R), `swP` (4W), `sdP` (8W) — and not `ldP` (8R). That is the pass-3 “sides taken somewhere” shape applied to the window: writes got both widths, reads got only `lw`.

`accept()` would already emit an `ld` at `PROBE_PC` for a `ldP` tuple (`op[:2] == "ld"`). The tuple is simply not in the list.

Every `at=PROBE_PC` site in the probe, accepted or refused:

- `rev pc ctl`: **lw** (4R)
- `lb`/`lh`/`sb`/`sh` **rev pc**: refused, never accepted
- `ACCEPTS`: `lwP`, `swP`, `sdP` only

No accepted 8-byte read of CRIME_REV is ever issued from the window.
