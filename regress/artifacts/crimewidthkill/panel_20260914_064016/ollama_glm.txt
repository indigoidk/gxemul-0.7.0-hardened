## Q3 -- the wrong statement

READ (header, DISCLOSED): "a decline answering a selection of the register's bytes at CRIME_REV, CRIME_CONTROL or CRIME_TIME that the model holds at 0 there -- **the ADDRESSED lanes (bytes 0-1 at a label)**, or the 4-byte half above the low word -- is EQUIVALENT."
