## 3. What all nine missed

Exact edit, `DEVICE_ACCESS(crime)` width `else`, one token of control flow:

```c
		/* was: goto crime_declined; */
		if (len != 2 || relative_addr != CRIME_INTSTAT)
			goto crime_declined;
```

Same size, same hole: `CRIME_INTMASK` or `CRIME_TIME` in place of `CRIME_INTSTAT`.

A refused `lh`/`lhu`/`sh` at `+0x10` now enters `case CRIME_INTSTAT:` and calls `crime_interrupt_reassert()`. After the same `sd INTMASK=bit28` / `sd SOFTINT=bit28` setup the F5 rows already use, that sets cause.IP2. The skip is gone at that cell; the announce and the `len==1` rows still pass.

**Row that should have caught it:** `istat lh` (and `istat sh`). **They do not exist.**
