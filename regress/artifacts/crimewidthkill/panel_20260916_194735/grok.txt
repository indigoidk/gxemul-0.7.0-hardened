### 1. The enumeration is right

The switch distinguishes exactly seven things: `CRIME_REV` (0x00), `CRIME_CONTROL` (0x08), `CRIME_INTSTAT` (0x10), `CRIME_INTMASK` (0x18), `CRIME_DOG` (0x30), `CRIME_TIME` (0x38), and `default`. `CRIME_SOFTINT` (0x20) and `CRIME_HARDINT` (0x28) are not case labels; they fall into `default`, which is how the generator wrote `C_dfl*`. INTSTAT and INTMASK share a body and are still two labels — table 11 killed them separately (`C_ist4r` vs `C_imk4r`).

Axes considered and **not** missing:

- **`relative_addr & 4`**. The 4-byte arm does branch on it. Every named label is 8-aligned, so `& 4` is always 0 there. At `default` both lanes exist and both are rowed (`p rev lw4` / `p dog rlo` / `p dog wlo` vs `p acc 40 *` / `top4 rhi`). A lane-restricted mutant is a subset of `C_dfl4*`, not a new product axis.
- **PROM-window `pc`**. The REV arm tests it. Out-of-window REV 8W / 4W-hi / 4R-hi are in the 10 EQUIVALENT (prelude rewrites `reg[0]`; high half of 0x11 is 0). The in-window half is ROWED (`p revP * pc`). `C_rev4w` / `C_rev4r` / `C_rev8w` / `C_rev8r` hit both and die on the window rows.
- **Idata at CONTROL**. That is ACCEPTS-entry granularity, not a 56-cell axis. `C_ctl4w` / `C_ctl8w` decline the whole label and red `p acc ctl msg`.

`{latch} × {4,8} × {R,W} × {7 labels}` = 56 is the right product.
