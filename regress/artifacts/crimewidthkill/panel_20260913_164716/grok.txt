## Q2 — is the repair sound?

Yes, as an instrument. Table 4 is the variance proof for the new rows; I did not re-run them.

- **Oracles vary** under the change they name. Every new row that PRETABLE 4 assigned a mutant reddened exactly that set (`check_pred.out`: `exact=57 differs=0`).
- **Controls are live** on the fixed build (`probe_first6_fix.out`: `CRIMEP_CONTROL=OK`, 111/111). `rev pc ctl` redirects, `sw08 halts` returns the sentinel, `sw08 msg ctl` prints, imask/istat pre/ctl flip IP2, time `* ctl` goes large (~2.8e8), `dflt ctl` prints, `gate hardint` is raw **32** (`CRIME_INT_PERIPH_MISC`), `gate line` is IP2 set.
- **TIME wording is now right.** `machine.c` sets `ticks_till_next[n] = 0` at registration; the 8-byte arm samples `d->reg[TIME]` *then* the switch updates. So s1 is the first tick’s 66–132 (this log: 132/132/66/132), s2 is the sampling `ld`’s wall-clock update. A second tick is `1<<14` steps later. The old “nothing else updates TIME” sentence is gone. Load can false-red, not false-green, as stated.
- **`top said` is the right channel for the 4R-high cell.** Value is 0 either way; `said(..., 1, 0x27b)` fails when the `lw +0x210` takes the session’s one notice. PRETABLE found that at writing time; table 4 measured it.

Not a vacuity, but the **gate comment welds two independent setups into one causal chain.** MACE `sd 0x01` at +0x10 then +0x18 does latch HARDINT to 0x20 (measured). The line is asserted because INTMASK and SOFTINT are `0x10000000` and an accepted `lw` reasserts — HARDINT bit 5 is **not** in that mask. `gate hardint` and `gate line` are both live; they are not cause and effect. `o_hi_gate` / `o_pa_gate` still red `gate run` for the reasons claimed.

## Q3 — wrong statements

**CONTRACT item 6 vs DISCLOSED, on the 4R cells at +0x00.** Item 6 says `devsig` / `devsig hi` *are* those cells. `devsig` is a 4R at **+0x04** (low half, 0x11 — real). `devsig hi` is a 4R at +0x00 of a value that is **0 accepted or refused**, so it cannot pin the cell. DISCLOSED two bullets later is the true sentence: the 4R-high cell is carried by `rev pc ctl` (the PROM hack needs the access accepted). Those two contract sentences cannot both stand.

Lesser, not load-bearing:

- CONTRACT 4’s `hi once` clause, “followed by refusals both >= 0x40 and < 0x200”. The follow-ups are +0x27c/+0x27e (`>= 0x200`) and +0x07 (`< 0x40`). Nothing in that session sits in `[0x40, 0x200)`. The row still works for the mutants it was built for (`o_lt_blk`, `lt_rng` as implemented).
- DISCLOSED still lists rowed accept offsets as +0x30, +0x210, +0x278. +0x00 is now rowed (`acc8 near`). The “strictly INSIDE the window” qualifier keeps the interior claim true; the offset list is stale.
- CONTRACT 3’s “fall-through keyed on ANY data bit” is true only of bits a 1- or 2-byte store can carry (0–7 / 0–15). Bits above 15 cannot be set on a refused `sb`/`sh`, so a conjunct on them is equivalent, not a miss.

Bank-1 at +0x210 for `-M 64` is right: `CRIME_MEM_BANK_CTRL0` is **0x208**, bank 1 is +0x210, prelude writes `1`. `top8 anchor` / `top4 anchor` both read `0x1` on the fixed build. (The header’s `CTRL2` name for 0x210 is the filed `crimebankorder` dispute, not a detector error.)

`#95` pre-zero is where DISCLOSED says it is (`memory_rw.c`, `memset` immediately above `paddr -= mem->devices[i].baseaddr`).
