### Q1 — the repair

**(a) Premises and partition**  
DERIVED from packet: none of S1-S24 is false on the inlined source.  
P1: no decision depends on whether bits 7/5 are “reserved” or “always 1” — the only consumer is the byte-exact readback.  
P2: the exact meaning of `sectorsize` is not needed for byte-exact readback, so the readback design does not depend on it.  
P3: “latent” changes timing/severity records, not whether to fix — the wrong-sector defect is real for a guest that ignores word 49.  
P4: after the fix, the model honours LBA while word 49 bit 9 is clear and words 60-61 are nonzero. That is an interim ATA-2 noncompliance, but less harmful than serving the wrong sector.

7.1 partition: mostly correct, but the row  
`bit 6 SET, LBA at or past the file's end (4,096 here)`  
is marked “changed? yes”. On the witness image every LBA >= 4096 gives HEAD CHS offset >= 16127, past advertised capacity 5040, so HEAD also returns zeros / refuses writes. FIX returns zeros / refuses writes as well. There is no observable change for that row on this image. It should be `no` or explicitly “both zero/refused”.

Outside the four fixed sites:  
- if readback mechanism (ii) is used, no product-code change outside the four sites is required;  
- if mechanism (i) is used, `struct wdc_data` gains a raw SDH field, and that is outside the four sites.

**(b) Decode and readback**  
DERIVED: recommend mechanism (ii):  
`d->sectorsize = idata & 0xa0;`  
`d->lba = (idata >> 6) & 1;`  
and compose readback as  
`odata = d->sectorsize | (d->lba << 6) | (d->drive << 4) | d->head;`  
This keeps the SDH readback byte-exact without adding a field.

For the variants:  
- V16, V17, V18 all fail under this mechanism as predicted.  
- The debug lines must stop calling `sectorsize` a size field, or print the raw SDH byte instead.

An added field is an ADDITION under R13. V17’s predicted `sdh rb2` 0x93 is not a measured delta and cannot licence the addition by itself; it must be measured on the fixed build. If `sectorsize` loses its last reader under mechanism (i), removing it is not in scope for this round — file it.

**(c) Arithmetic and two sites**  
DERIVED: use a 64-bit product at both sites, e.g.  
`offset = 512ULL * (uint64_t)(((d->head & 0xf) << 24) + (cyl << 8) + d->sector);`  
or a shared helper returning `uint64_t`. The existing `printf` calls must be deleted, not converted to unconditional `printf`; a `debug` replacement is acceptable only if quiet_mode is respected.

Control flow check:  
- `wdc__read()` sets `offset` before the chunk loop; the loop increments `offset += 512 * to_read`; the `break` path still reaches `d->int_assert = 1;`.  
- `wdc__write()` sets `write_offset` after the LBA block, so V20 is a real ordering risk; the three assignments must remain after the LBA arm.

**(d) Option set**
