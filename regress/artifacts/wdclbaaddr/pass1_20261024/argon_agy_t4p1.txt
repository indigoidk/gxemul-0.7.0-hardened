### Q1(b) The Decode and Read-Back (`D5, P2, R13`)

- **Evaluation of mechanisms `(i)–(iv)` (`READ`)**:
  - Look at [`case wd_sdh:`](file:///C:/DocumentNoSnc/cc/GXEMUL/GXEMUL-SEC/src/devices/dev_wdc.c#L2006-L2022) (`READ`):
    - At `HEAD`, `(d->sectorsize << 6) + (d->lba << 5) + (d->drive << 4) + d->head` is byte-exact for all 256 values `0x00..0xff` **only** because `d->sectorsize` holds bits `7..6` and `d->lba` holds bit `5` (`S1`).
    - Once `d->lba = (idata >> 6) & 1;`, leaving the read-back formula untouched (`V17`) drops bit 5 on write and duplicates bit 6 into bit 5 on read-back (`0xb3 -> 0x93`, `0x4c -> 0x6c`).
  - **Best mechanism: `(ii)` (repurpose `d->sectorsize` in-place to hold the non-decoded bits 7 and 5, `idata & 0xa0`)**:
    - On write (`MEM_WRITE`):
      - `d->sectorsize = idata & 0xa0;` (or keep the 2-bit value `((idata >> 6) & 2) | ((idata >> 5) & 1)` if a 2-bit integer is preferred, though `idata & 0xa0` is direct)
      - `d->lba = (idata >> 6) & 1;`
      - `d->drive = (idata >> 4) & 1;`
      - `d->head = idata & 0xf;`
    - On read (`MEM_READ`):
      - `odata = (d->sectorsize & 0xa0) | (d->lba << 6) | (d->drive << 4) | d->head;`
  - **Why `(ii)` beats `(i)`, `(iii)`, and `(iv)` (`R13`)**:
    1. **Zero struct changes**: Unlike `(iii)`, it adds no field to [`struct wdc_data`](file:///C:/DocumentNoSnc/cc/GXEMUL/GXEMUL-SEC/src/devices/dev_wdc.c#L106-L154) and removes no field (`P2`, `R13`).
    2. **Orthogonal partition of the 8 bits**: `{7, 5}` (`d->sectorsize`), `{6}` (`d->lba << 6`), `{4}` (`d->drive << 4`), and `{3..0}` (`d->head`) are disjoint bit masks whose union is `0xff`.
    3. **Byte-exact across all 256 inputs (`0x00..0xff`)**: Kills `V16` (which fails `N4`: `0x4c -> 0xec`), `V17` (which fails `sdh rb2`: `0xb3 -> 0x93` and `N4`: `0x4c -> 0x6c`), and `V18` (which fails `sdh rb`: `0xe5 -> 0xa5` and `N4`: `0x4c -> 0x0c`).
    4. **Compatibility with `R13`**: Preserving byte-exact SDH read-back is not an uninvited feature addition—`HEAD` *already* has byte-exact SDH read-back (`S1`), so breaking it (`V17`) would be a regression introduced by this round's own edit.
    5. **Debug lines (`2010–2012, 2018–2020`)**: Update the `debug()` format in `case wd_sdh:` to print `lba=%i` (or `sdh=0x%02x, lba=%i, drive=%i, head=%i`) so trace output matches the decoded bit.

### Q1(c) The Arithmetic and the Two Sites (`D2, D3, P8, R11`)

1. **64-bit widening is mandatory in this round (`D2`, `P8`, `S3`, `S6`, `V15`) (`READ`)**:
   - In [`wdc__read()`](file:///C:/DocumentNoSnc/cc/GXEMUL/GXEMUL-SEC/src/devices/dev_wdc.c#L472-L474) and [`wdc__write()`](file:///C:/DocumentNoSnc/cc/GXEMUL/GXEMUL-SEC/src/devices/dev_wdc.c#L564-L566), `d->head & 0xf` is bits `27..24` of the LBA28 sector number.
   - Multiplying by `512` (`<< 9`) places `d->head & 0xf` at bits **`36..33`** of the byte offset.
   - In 32-bit `int`, bits `36..33` **do not exist**: for **every** `d->head != 0` (`1..15`), `512 * (((d->head & 0xf) << 24) + (cyl << 8) + d->sector)` overflows 32-bit signed `int` (undefined behavior in C) and, in 32-bit two's-complement arithmetic, shifts `(d->head & 0xf)` completely out of the 32-bit word (`((head & 0xf) << 33) & 0xffffffff == 0`)!
   - Worse still, for `head = 1, cyl = 0, sector = 5` (`N1`, `N2`), 32-bit wrap yields `offset = 2560` (sector 5), which **bypasses `#416`'s bounds check** in [`diskimage__internal_access()`](file:///C:/DocumentNoSnc/cc/GXEMUL/GXEMUL-SEC/src/disk/diskimage.c#L1591-L1601) (`S8`) and silently reads or overwrites sector 5 of the 4,096-sector disk!
   - Therefore, both sites **must** widen the product to 64 bits in this round, matching the `(int64_t)` idiom already used on the CHS line immediately above them ([`dev_wdc.c:466-468, 559-561`](file:///C:/DocumentNoSnc/cc/GXEMUL/GXEMUL-SEC/src/devices/dev_wdc.c#L466-L468)):
     `offset = (int64_t)512 * (((d->head & 0xf) << 24) + (cyl << 8) + d->sector);`
   - Keeping the two inline `if (d->lba)` blocks (rather than refactoring `wdc__read`/`wdc__write` into a helper) preserves the local structure of both functions and matches the 4-site floor accounting (`7.2(b)`, `V14`).

2. **The `printf` calls (`475, 567`; `D3`) (`READ`)**:
   - The `printf("LBA offset = %lli\n", (long long)offset);` lines inside `#if 0` ([`dev_wdc.c:475, 567`](file:///C:/DocumentNoSnc/cc/GXEMUL/GXEMUL-SEC/src/devices/dev_wdc.c#L475)) **must not** be enabled as `printf`: they would spam host `stdout` on every LBA sector read/write and corrupt console output in `drive_guest.py` / `Emu` sessions. Delete them (or convert them to `debug()`; note [`wdc_command()`](file:///C:/DocumentNoSnc/cc/GXEMUL/GXEMUL-SEC/src/devices/dev_wdc.c#L970-L981) already logs every READ/WRITE command via `debug()`).

3. **Control-flow trace (`R11`, `S7`, `8.3`, `8.4`, `8.8`) (`READ`)**:
   - **`wdc__read(struct wdc_data *d, int count)` ([`dev_wdc.c:459-549`](file:///C:/DocumentNoSnc/cc/GXEMUL/GXEMUL-SEC/src/devices/dev_wdc.c#L459-L549))**:

     2. With `if (d->lba)` enabled (`:472-474`), `offset` is overwritten with `(int64_t)512 * (((d->head & 0xf) << 24) + (cyl << 8) + d->sector)`, which lies in `[0, (2^28 - 1) * 512] = [0, 0x1fffffff00]` (always non-negative when passed as `off_t` / `int64_t` to `diskimage_access`).
     3. Enters `while (count > 0)` (`:478`), enforces the `#484` ring occupancy cap (`:514-528`), calls `diskimage_access(d->cpu->machine, d->drive, DISKIMAGE_IDE, 0, offset, buf, 512 * to_read)` (`:531-533`), pushes big-endian 16-bit halfwords into `d->read_buf` via `wdc_put_in_read_buf(d, ...)` (`:501-505`), increments `offset += 512 * to_read` (`:544`), and sets `d->int_assert = 1` (`:548`).

### Q1(d) The Option Set (`R12, P18`)

- **Member of the `R12` option set**: **Member 7 (`IMPLEMENT`)** (and **Member 4 (`ACCEPT`)** for bit 6 of `wd_sdh`). Under `R12`, options 1–6 (`CLAMP`, `MASK`, `IGNORE`, `ACCEPT`, `FAULT`, `DECLINE`) must not be chosen by reflex when the unimplemented path is a small, bounded calculation that the file already sketched under `#if 0`.
- **Why `FAULT` (`R12` member 5) is not appropriate and what `DECLINE` looks like here (`P18`, `READ`)**:
  - Architectural bus fault (`return 0` from `DEVICE_ACCESS(wdc)`, which raises `EXCEPTION_DBE` on MIPS, as documented in the `#494`/`#495` comments at [`dev_wdc.c:750-757, 1547-1553`](file:///C:/DocumentNoSnc/cc/GXEMUL/GXEMUL-SEC/src/devices/dev_wdc.c#L750-L757)) is **not** how ATA controllers report command errors and would crash or wedge a guest kernel in an unrecoverable bus-error trap.
  - Controller-level command refusal in `dev_wdc.c` is `DECLINE` (`R12` member 6 / `P18(b)`): setting `d->error |= WDCE_ABRT; d->int_assert = 1;` and returning without setting `WDCS_DRQ`, as the no-disk check ([`dev_wdc.c:947-954`](file:///C:/DocumentNoSnc/cc/GXEMUL/GXEMUL-SEC/src/devices/dev_wdc.c#L947-L954)), ATAPI `WDCC_IDENTIFY` ([`dev_wdc.c:955-962`](file:///C:/DocumentNoSnc/cc/GXEMUL/GXEMUL-SEC/src/devices/dev_wdc.c#L955-L962)), `SET_FEATURES` default (`:1000`), `WDCC_SEC_*` (`:1110`), and `default:` (`:1155`) do (`READ`).
- **Why honouring LBA (`P18(a)`) beats aborting with `WDCE_ABRT` (`P18(b)`)**:
  1. ATA-2 (`S12`, section 9.1 lines 1850–1851) states *"A host shall not attempt to use LBA addressing unless the device indicates the mode is supported"*; it does **not** require a device to abort bit-6 commands when word 49 bit 9 is 0 (especially when words 60–61 are already non-zero, `S10`).
  2. Real guests that issue LBA commands without checking word 49 bit 9 (e.g. FreeBSD `ata`/`ada`, `1.5`, and Linux `ide-disk` via `lba_capacity_is_ok()`, `S23`) work when LBA28 is honoured (`P18(a)`) and fail if aborted (`P18(b)`), whereas CHS guests (`NetBSD`, `OpenBSD`) leave bit 6 clear and never enter the `if (d->lba)` arm.
  3. `P18`'s four-option list `(a)–(d)` is complete for command-level dispositions.

## Q4: Disagreements `D1–D9` (`Section 3.5`)

- **`D1` (Bit-6 decode vs LBA offset blocks)**: **Ship both together in this round.** Enabling the LBA28 offset blocks with `d->lba` still decoded from bit 5 (`(idata >> 5) & 1`) misroutes every standard CHS command (`0xa0 | head`, bit 5 = 1) into LBA addressing (`S1, V1`), while fixing bit 6 alone leaves `d->lba` unread (`S2, V14, R23`).
- **`D2` (32-bit vs 64-bit LBA product)**: **Widen to 64-bit (`(int64_t)512 * ...`) in this round.** Every LBA request with `d->head != 0` (`1..15`) has `((d->head & 0xf) << 24) >= 2^24 > 2^22`, which overflows 32-bit signed `int` (UB) and shifts `head` completely out of 32-bit arithmetic, bypassing `#416`'s bounds check (`S3, S6, P8, V15, N1, N2`).
- **`D3` (`printf("LBA offset...")` in `#if 0`)**: **Delete the `printf` lines (or convert to `debug()`).** Host `stdout` `printf` on every disk read/write pollutes guest serial transcripts and breaks console harnesses (`S2, R13`).
- **`D4` (IDENTIFY word 49 and `row_no_lba_claim()`)**: **Keep IDENTIFY word 49 = 0 in this round; keep `row_no_lba_claim()` with its comment updated and add a rung-1 LBA row in `diff_wdc_identify.c` alongside rung-3 `wdc_lba_probe.py`.** Flipping word 49 changes guest OS behavior across the fleet and is a separate round (`S4, S10, S12, S13, S22, P4, C18, R15, R17, R23`).
- **`D5` (`wd_sdh` read-back and `d->sectorsize`)**: **Preserve byte-exact read-back across `0x00..0xff` by storing `d->sectorsize = idata & 0xa0` and composing `odata = (d->sectorsize & 0xa0) | (d->lba << 6) | (d->drive << 4) | d->head`.** Adds zero struct fields, preserves `HEAD`'s byte-exact SDH read-back invariant, and kills `V16, V17, V18` with `sdh rb`, `sdh rb2`, and `N4` (`S1, S4, P1, P2, C15–C17, N4, R1(b), R13`).
- **`D6` (CHS `sector == 0` guard)**: **Do not add a `d->sector == 0` guard on the CHS path.** `#416` (`S8`) already zero-fills/refuses `offset = -512` at `(sector=0, head=0, cyl=0)`, and adding an unrequested CHS guard violates `R13` (`S5, S8, P3, R13`).
- **`D7` (Gate wiring)**: **Wire `wdc_lba_probe.py` into `regress/check_probe_wiring.py` (`STANDALONE`) and `regress/gate_offline.sh` (Gate 10) in the same commit, with single-binary default CLI mode (`python3 regress/wdc_lba_probe.py "$GXEMUL_BIN"`).** Required by `R15` and enforced automatically by `check_probe_wiring.py` (`P15, R15`).
- **`D8` (Stale comments and docs)**: **Update `diff_wdc_identify.c:5613-5616`, `gate_offline.sh:1890`, `CHANGELOG.md`, and `OUTSTANDING_BUGS.md` in the same commit; leave dated `regress/artifacts/research_20261022/*` files untouched.** Live gate/test comments must not contradict the tree (`S4, D8`).
