That mechanism is usable from these callbacks because:

- `X(mcr_mrc)` synchronizes `cpu->pc` before calling the dispatcher.
- `ARM_EXCEPTION_UND` maps to the implemented `ARM_MODE_UND32` exception arm.
- `arm_exception()` saves status, installs the exception register bank and return address, selects the vector, and redirects translated execution.
- The caller performs no subsequent result assignment that would overwrite the exception outcome.
