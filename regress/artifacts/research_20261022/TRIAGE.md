# GXemul fork -- one triage of every STILL OPEN item in research reports A, B and C

Date 2026-10-22.  Fork: `GXEMUL-SEC` at HEAD `de8c7547` (all `file:line` are under `GXEMUL-SEC/src/` unless a path says otherwise).  Inputs, read completely: `A_github_forks.md` (46 repos, 367 rows), `B_distro_patches.md` (18 distros, 28 patches), `C_reports_upstream.md` (upstream SVN after 0.7.0, lists, trackers; 61 findings).  Read-only on the project.

**Method.** (1) Every row a report marks STILL OPEN / OPEN / PARTIAL was taken; rows describing one defect were merged and keep every source URL.  Rows a report marks UNCERTAIN/UNVERIFIED are listed separately and not counted.  (2) Filing was checked mechanically: `triage_work/search.py` greps `OUTSTANDING_BUGS.md` (25,204 lines) and every `ledger.json` row (941 rows; id, title, notes, entries) for each defect's file/function/device/instruction keywords; every hit was then read (`triage_work/ctx.py`, outputs in `triage_work/search_out.txt`).  FILED means a row's id, title or governing text names the defect; PART means a row covers a named part only; UNFILED means no row has it as its subject -- a mention as context or in id-less prose is recorded in the cell but counts as UNFILED.  (3) Ranking keys, in order: class tier (HOST-STOP and GUEST-CORRUPTION, then WRONG-RESULT, CPU-STOP, HYGIENE/BUILD, FEATURE); reach (U user mode > U* user mode but needs a non-default option > K guest kernel/firmware/device registers > K* kernel but needs -X or similar > C configuration, debugger, host environment or a non-default build > B build only); fix to port (Y yes, possibly with a stated change > P partial or idea only > N design); then two keys of mine -- impact (1 broad, on a booted rig, or silent data damage; 2 one machine or guest family; 3 minor) and unfiled before filed.  CPU-STOP includes device accesses and firmware calls that clear `cpu->running`.  Line numbers are the reports' (re-read here for the top rows); `dev_sh4.c` is the only cited file with uncommitted working-tree edits.
