**Q1 — The diff: right and complete?**
Right: yes. The walk `for (cpu = 0; cpu < MAX_CPUS; cpu++)` calling `INTERRUPT_ASSERT(d->cpu_irq[cpu])` / `INTERRUPT_DEASSERT(d->cpu_irq[cpu])` through NULL pointers on slots 1–3 when `ncpus < 2/3/4` is the stated mechanism, and bounding the loop to `d->ncpus` (the count of actually-CONNECTed slots) eliminates the only route by which the walk reaches an unpopulated slot. All three callers enumerated (the two store arms, the SIO line, the clock tick) go through the same `reassert_interrupts(d)`; bounding the walk bounds all of them. M_CALLGUARD in the table confirms the call-site form is insufficient because the SIO/clock route bypasses the store arms — this is the load-bearing reason option (a) is the right member of the six-membered set, not a warrant preference.

Complete modulo one robustness point: `d->ncpus = devinit->machine->ncpus` is placed BEFORE the `ncpus > 4` exit. The packet asserts the exit "ends the process at init time" (settled premise 3) and the diff comment leans on that. Pre-fix, the walk was `cpu < MAX_CPUS`, so an `ncpus==5` configuration could not buffer-overflow regardless of the exit; post-fix, `d->ncpus==5` would make the walk read `interrupt_enable[4]` / `irqActive[4]` / `cpu_irq[4]` / `software_interrupt_status[4]` — all out of bounds. The exit is now load-bearing for correctness, where before it was a sanity check. Fable's pass-1 recommendation (clamp, or move the capture after the exit) was not taken. **NOT CHECKABLE FROM PACKET:** whether the exit is `exit(1)` / fatal-return or a warning that continues. The brief's wording ("ends the process at init time") and the diff comment both assert the former; if true, the diff is fine. If the exit is ever softened, the fix regresses to a buffer overrun. **Single fact that would settle it:** the body of the `ncpus > 4` arm in DEVINIT (one grep). A two-line clamp (`d->ncpus = n < MAX_CPUS ? n : MAX_CPUS;`) would make this concern go away at zero behavioral cost.

The store arms are intentionally untouched (MASK1–3 / SOFT_INT1–3 stay guest-writable storage with readback); pass-1 ruling is consistent with the lunaabsentmaskdecl follow-up. mask1/mask2/mask3 readback rows exercise this.

The (d) set is the predicate `cpu < d->ncpus` plus the five fields the body reads; joint cells are {slot 0..3} × {pending ∈ {sw, device-line}} × {irqActive ∈ {0, 1}}. The eleven mutants in the table cover:

The row exists; the cell is covered. **CLOSE.** I do not see a clean-sweep mutant the detector fails to catch within the reachable configuration space (ncpus ∈ {1, 2}).
