For Q2, the proposed single rule is:

> **For probe entries added or changed after CUTOFF = 2026-09-09, require either CLASS_DETECTOR with a derived gate invocation, a valid unexpired EXEMPT naming the round owing its detector wiring, or an ungated CLASS_WITNESS explicitly declaring “post-fix green NEVER measured” and a pre-fix-symptom oracle; report unchanged on/before-cutoff violations as archived debt.**

I would use **structured MANIFEST fields**, covering the declaration date, post-fix-green status, oracle polarity, and evidence reference. The note remains readable explanation. The round must migrate existing declarations by inspecting their notes and cited evidence.

The three passing dispositions are exact:

1. **Wired detector:** `CLASS_DETECTOR`, repaired-property oracle, and an invocation derived from the battery’s scripts. A claimed gate name in the note does not suffice.
2. **Deferred detector:** a valid calendar expiry date, not yet expired, and an explicit owing-round ID. To satisfy Q3’s “same entry” control, a measured-green entry may temporarily retain `CLASS_WITNESS`, but must render as **exempt detector debt**. This requires narrowly changing today’s orphan-exemption rejection; true symptom witnesses must remain ineligible.
3. **True witness:** `CLASS_WITNESS`, no gate invocation, an explicit “post-fix green NEVER measured” declaration, and a declaration that its assertions expect the pre-fix symptom and would reject the repair. An unmeasured repaired-property probe cannot use this route.

The checker must enumerate every relevant entry and require the fields, rather than select entries whose prose happens to match a regex. Missing or unknown live declarations fail explicitly. Its summary must account for every entry as enforced or archived; zero enforced entries must visibly mean “all archived.”

Because MANIFEST currently has no entry dates, cutoff migration also needs a frozen inventory of the existing entry/probe versions. Only those unchanged versions qualify for archival treatment; a new entry cannot obtain grandfathering by supplying an old date. Missing dates outside that inventory are new, following the bugfile precedent.

This cannot prove that a human’s `NEVER` declaration is truthful. It does prevent an omitted marker or unfamiliar wording from silently removing an entry from consideration.
