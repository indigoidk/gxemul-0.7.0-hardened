REASONED: therefore any edit confined to the shown PORT arm fails to explain the red set. Session-death at instruction 1 or 2 of A is excluded because every process-ender reachable by an offset-0 write/read would kill C identically. Conclusion: either (a) M_acc4 edits code the packet never shows (and the "22 of 22 … of the fix's own predicate, store and latch" sentence is then wrong about it), or (b) `current_port` is *not* validly initialized at power-on — in which case the row is explained, but two added comment sentences are false ("keeps its previous, always-valid value"; "unreachable while the PORT arm refuses" — the DATA tripwire would be reachable once at power-on), the NOT CLAIMED list is missing a third live kill path, and pass 1's "always-valid, unanimous" premise was wrong. **One fact settles all of this: the DEV_INIT/malloc lines of dev_fbctrl.c plus M_acc4's diff in the evidence directory.** Name it, read it, then write the records accordingly.
