B1-B2: Read BT instead of BB.

At cpu_ppc_instr.c:2733 or :2760, change:
    bb = (cpu->cd.ppc.cr >> (31-bb)) & 1;
to:
    bb = (cpu->cd.ppc.cr >> (31-bt)) & 1;

Let r be the architectural result. Every exercised destination still holds 1-r when its instruction starts. The substituted read therefore makes !bb equal r. The resulting functions are a & r = r for crandc and a | r = r for crorc. Both edited handlers consequently produce the expected destination and preserve every other bit.
