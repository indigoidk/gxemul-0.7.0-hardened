### 2. Which of my accepted dispositions is wrong?
**P7 is wrong.** Changing the text to "the one NUMERIC input" to exclude NaN is imprecise. In IEEE 754, while NaN stands for "Not a Number", it is still a valid state of the floating-point data type. The strictly accurate term to exclude Inf and NaN is "FINITE input".

If the upstream code accidentally copy-pasted `FTRC`'s integer-truncation store into `FCNVSD` (which should only *read* FPUL), the panel completely missed an audit of its sister instruction, **`FCNVDS`** (Convert Double to Single). `FCNVDS` legitimately *writes* to FPUL.
