[REASONED] Let:

- `E = int_enable_mask & R4030_EXT_IP3`;

|---|---:|---|---|---:|---|

| 1 | 1 | Deassert | Leave asserted | Yes | Not cleanly |
| 2 | 0 | Deassert | Deassert | No | No |

- `jz ip one` observes the one-source state before a vector read.

[REASONED] The literal claim that the line-pending/mask-disabled cell is “unobservable through the tail’s predicate” is false:
