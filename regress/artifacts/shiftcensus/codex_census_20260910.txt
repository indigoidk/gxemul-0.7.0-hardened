**The eight unresolved operators**

**The 16-bit-`unsigned int` qualification**

`1U` is not a universal replacement. The already-unsigned ARM UBFX/SBFX masks, M88K `1U << d`, generated ARM carry masks, GD-ROM/SH4 warning masks, and LCA’s `0x800U << dev` can exceed a 16-bit unsigned operand’s width.

There are also numerous plain-`int` assumptions, including `1 << DYNTRANS_L1N` with count 17. Consequently, the `NONE` totals above are explicitly **not an arbitrary-C99-host portability certificate**. For repairs intended to support the architectural 32-bit mask independently of `unsigned int` width, use the architectural-width operand before shifting, together with any necessary count or field validation.

Finally, one definite additional finding deserves its own correctness row: [PPC `X(rldicl)`](C:/DocumentNoSnc/CC/GXEMUL/GXEMUL-SEC/src/cpus/cpu_ppc_instr.c) initializes `tmp2 = 0`, ORs the mask into **`tmp`**, and assigns `tmp & tmp2` to the destination. It therefore **always writes zero**. The computed count is safe; the mask is accumulated into the wrong variable. This is **CRITICAL and EASY**, independently of the shift-UB census.
