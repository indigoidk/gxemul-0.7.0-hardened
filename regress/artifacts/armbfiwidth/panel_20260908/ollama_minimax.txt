### Issues I noticed in the packet

**1. Citation 6.5.7p4 for `1<<31` being UB is incorrect.** [REASONED] C99/C11 6.5.7p4 does not make `1<<31` UB: with 32-bit `int`, E2=31 < 32 (not UB by p3), E1=1 is non-negative (not UB by p4). The shifted value `0x80000000` is `INT_MIN`, representable. The actual UB at `width==31` arises from the **subtraction**: `(1<<31) - 1` is signed integer overflow on the `- 1`. **The conclusion (`width==31` needs fixing) is correct; the cited clause is wrong.** This matters only for citation hygiene, not for the fix.

**2. Denominator does not reconcile cleanly.** [REASONED] Brief claims 1024 encodable pairs, 468 reaching UB, 465 of those with `msb<lsb`. My count: 496 pairs have `msb<lsb`; 30 of those have `width=0` (defined, since `1<<0`=1); 466 have `width<0` (UB). Plus 1 pair at `width==32` (UB by p3). Plus 2 pairs at `width==31` (shift defined per standard, but `(1<<31)-1` UB from the subtraction). Total UB-class: 467 (excluding the subtraction-UB at width==31) or 469 (including). The brief's 468 is off by 1–2 from either count. **The qualitative conclusion (most UB is `msb<lsb`) is sound; the number is approximate.** [REASONED]
